Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 10 review replies every month and never asks for a card.

What exactly does Rosalia do?

On the Free plan, she replies to up to 10 reviews a month. On the Mini plan, she replies to up to 100 reviews, suggests what to post each week and on special dates, and prepares 4 photos from your own pictures every month. She also looks after your profile: opening hours, holidays, phone and WhatsApp link, with suggestions on what's missing and statistics on how people find you.

Will Rosalia publish things without asking me?

Rosalia replies to good reviews on her own, so you don't have to. Anything sensitive, like a 1 to 3 star review or a new post, waits for your OK on WhatsApp. Change or switch off anything, anytime.

What do I need?

An email address, a Google Business Profile and WhatsApp.

What does the Mini plan cost?

R179 a month for review replies, posts and photos together. You only pay if you choose to upgrade.
