A Facebook posting service that keeps your Page useful

BabyRock turns owner photos, events and opening hours into approved Facebook Page posts, then publishes them on a sustainable rhythm. Posting is an optional paid add-on: Content 12 is 9.99 EUR a month for 12 publications, and Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR a month.

Why BabyRock

Choose BabyRock for a Page built from real business material with one approval loop. Use Content 12 for a light rhythm or Content Unlimited for heavier publishing.

A Facebook Page that grows from your real business week

A useful Page grows from real material: a finished job, a class date, a change in opening hours or a question customers keep asking. Send photos and a short note through WhatsApp. BabyRock drafts a Facebook-ready caption, keeps practical details clear and places the post in the publishing plan for your approval.

Plan Page posts around the dates customers need

Facebook is where people check practical details: when you open, what happens this week and whether an event is still running. Build the month around repeatable post types so the Page stays useful. A schedule post explains a class, a service post shows what customers can book, and a behind-the-scenes photo makes the team familiar. BabyRock adapts each caption to its job, from a practical announcement to a personal behind-the-scenes update.

Announce a month of workshops from one owner update

A repair workshop runs four beginner sessions a month. The owner sends a workbench photo, class dates, price and one note explaining who the session suits. BabyRock turns that into an early announcement, a beginner-focused explanation, a reminder and a final call for remaining places. The owner checks the details in WhatsApp, corrects one date and approves the rest. The Page supports the classes without the owner writing four captions.

Approve the caption, image and timing in one pass

Every post goes through you before publication. Approve the wording and image together, request a clearer opening or correct a date or price. Approved posts move into the schedule and publish to the connected Facebook Page. Content 12 provides 12 publications a month, enough for three posts a week with room for an event. Content Unlimited is the paid option for a heavier cadence. The free plan keeps public comment replies and DM alerts in the same owner-friendly setup.

Choose Content 12 or Content Unlimited for a steady Page

Start with the job you need done. If the Page has been quiet and you want a credible weekly presence, Content 12 gives you a defined allowance and approval routine. If classes, launches or several locations create more updates, Content Unlimited removes the 12-publication ceiling. Both are paid add-ons beside BabyRock's social reply workflow, keeping planned posts and incoming engagement in one owner-friendly setup.

Questions buyers ask

Is there a done-for-you Facebook posting service?

Yes. BabyRock offers an optional paid posting service built around owner photos and notes. You approve posts before publication.

How many Facebook posts are included?

Content 12 includes 12 publications a month for 9.99 EUR. Content Unlimited costs 29.99 EUR a month for a heavier cadence.

Can I use the same photos for Instagram and Facebook?

Yes. Use the same photo set, then adjust the crop and caption for each platform.

Do I approve posts before publication?

Yes. Review the caption and image in WhatsApp and request changes before approved posts are scheduled.

Is Facebook posting part of the free plan?

Posting is a paid Content 12 or Content Unlimited add-on. The free plan focuses on public comment replies and DM alerts.

How does the Meta connection affect setup?

Content work can start now; live publishing begins when the account connection is active.

What do you need from me each month?

A few usable photos and a short note with dates, services or opening hours. Clear details speed up approval.
