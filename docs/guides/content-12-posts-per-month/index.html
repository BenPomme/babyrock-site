Content 12: twelve done-for-you social posts a month

BabyRock Content 12 is a paid add-on that turns your photos, notes and business updates into 12 owner-approved Instagram and Facebook publications a month for 9.99 EUR. It gives a small business a defined publishing rhythm without hiring a content team.

Why BabyRock

Choose BabyRock Content 12 for a steady, light social presence with one simple monthly allowance. Choose Content Unlimited at 29.99 EUR when your calendar needs a heavier publishing cadence.

A month of planned publications handled by Content 12

Content 12 covers 12 publications a month. Send raw material through WhatsApp: photos, dates, opening hours, offers or common questions. BabyRock turns it into captions, formats images for the selected channel and places each post in the publishing plan. The package supports a consistent monthly rhythm from repeatable material. It gives the owner clear slots to fill and keeps approval with the person who knows the details.

One four-week workflow from photos to approved posts

Before the month starts, agree the main dates, offers and events, then map 12 posts across four weeks. The owner supplies enough photos and notes for the whole plan, so approval has context. The first week establishes the pattern. Weeks two and three keep the Page active while the owner notes customer questions. The final week completes the allowance and reviews the next month. The value is a steady publishing cadence built from real business material.

Help a local shop turn 12 photos into a full month

A homeware shop has 12 usable photos but no plan. BabyRock gives each image a distinct job: new stock, a care tip, the owner's favourite piece, a staff pick, a customer question, weekend hours, local delivery, gift ideas, a maker story and a month-end recap. Two owner notes add a delivery delay and a workshop detail. The result is a month of Page activity from existing material.

Keep owner approval at the centre of publishing

Every publication waits for owner approval. You see the caption, image and intended channel in WhatsApp. Approve it, request a clearer opening or correct a price or date. Approved posts then move into the schedule for the connected channel.

Choose Content 12 or Content Unlimited for your month

Content 12 fits when three posts a week is enough. It provides a fixed allowance at 9.99 EUR a month and keeps monthly planning simple. Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR a month for more frequent publishing, several promotions or a larger stream of updates. Both are paid add-ons that sit alongside free public comment replies and DM alerts.

Questions buyers ask

What is the Content 12 add-on?

Content 12 is a paid publishing service for 12 owner-approved Instagram and Facebook publications a month. You supply the photos and notes; BabyRock prepares the posts.

How much does Content 12 cost?

Content 12 costs 9.99 EUR a month. Content Unlimited, for a heavier publishing cadence, costs 29.99 EUR a month.

Are the 12 posts split across Instagram and Facebook?

The allowance is 12 publications, so each scheduled publication uses one slot. Agree the Instagram and Facebook mix before the month starts so the plan matches your channels.

Do I need to write the captions?

No. Send photos and short notes. BabyRock drafts the captions, and you can request changes or correct details before approval.

Can I approve posts on WhatsApp?

Yes. Review the caption, image and intended channel in WhatsApp, then approve or request a revision.

What happens if I need more than 12 posts?

Move to Content Unlimited at 29.99 EUR a month for a heavier cadence. It is the paid option for businesses that need more than the Content 12 allowance.

When can live social publishing begin?

Content preparation and approval can start now. Live Instagram and Facebook publishing begins when the account connection is active.
