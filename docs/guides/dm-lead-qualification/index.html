Turn inbound social DMs into qualified conversations

BabyRock helps a business understand an inbound prospect before the owner checks a quote or availability. Owner-approved questions collect the useful details, the answers arrive with the conversation and every commercial decision stays with the person who can commit. The free plan includes DM alerts; paid social adds automatic inbound replies.

Why BabyRock

Choose BabyRock to answer new inquiries faster, collect the details that shape a quote and keep the final decision with the owner.

Turn a customer-started DM into a clear next step

Qualification begins when a prospect sends a DM. The first reply can acknowledge the inquiry and ask a few approved questions before the owner invests time in a custom answer.

The customer makes the first move, and the reply stays inside that conversation. The exchange respects consent while giving the business enough context to help.

Use three useful questions to make the owner's reply faster

Ask for the service or product, the timing or location and the detail that changes the answer. A photographer might ask for the date, venue and coverage. A repair business might ask for the item, fault and collection time.

Three questions are usually enough to route a conversation. Keep the form short, remove duplicate questions and focus on the detail that changes the owner's answer.

Handle a wedding date inquiry with clear owner control

A couple sends an Instagram DM asking whether a Saturday in June is available. The paid inbound flow acknowledges the message and asks for the date, venue and coverage. The couple answers in one thread.

The owner receives an alert with the context and checks the studio diary. The owner then replies with availability and a quote, or asks for a call. The couple speaks to the person making the commitment.

Send quotes, availability and exceptions straight to the owner

Questions about price, custom scope, a tight deadline or an unusual request should reach the owner. The same applies to discounts, brief changes or anything outside the approved workflow.

BabyRock sends an owner alert with the prospect's answers, then the owner checks the quote or availability and replies personally. Paid social starts at 9.99 EUR monthly for 200 conversations, or 24.99 EUR for 1,000.

Give every prospect an easy route to a person

Every flow should make it easy to stop answering questions and speak to a person. Use direct language, keep the pace comfortable and carry forward the details the prospect already shared. Complaints and urgent requests go straight to the owner.

After the first week, review where prospects stopped, which answers helped and which questions confused them. Refine the flow until it feels like a helpful reply and a clear route to the owner.

Questions buyers ask

Can Instagram DMs qualify leads automatically?

Yes, on a paid plan. BabyRock can ask owner-approved questions after a prospect messages first, then hand the context to the owner for the commercial decision.

What questions should a business ask in a DM?

Ask what the prospect needs, when and where they need it and the detail that changes the quote. Three focused questions are usually better than a long form.

When does an automated qualification conversation begin?

It begins after the prospect contacts the business and continues inside that DM thread, giving the owner useful context before a personal reply.

Can I limit automation to inbound conversations?

Yes. Replies follow conversations that people start with the business. The owner can approve the questions and escalation rules before launch.

How does the owner take over?

The owner receives the conversation and the prospect's answers, then replies personally for availability, quotes or exceptions.

Is DM lead qualification a paid feature?

The free plan includes DM alerts. Automatic inbound replies start at 9.99 EUR per month for 200 conversations, with the Grow plan at 24.99 EUR for a larger allowance.
