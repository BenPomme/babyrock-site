Guides

Google reviews, explained without the jargon.

Ten short guides for a shop that wants its listing to look alive. The same answers we give our own clients.

How to reply to every Google review

An unanswered review stays on your listing. The next person who searches for you will see it. This guide is the weekly habit: short, genuine replies, in the language of the review.

How to ask for Google reviews without breaking the rules

You may invite every customer, in the same way, to leave a genuine review. You may not pay for stars, filter the happy ones onto Google, or lean on people while they are still in the chair.

How to put a Google review QR code on your tickets

Google will give you a short link and a QR code for your listing. Print it. You do not need a phone list or messages to people you barely know.

When to ask for a Google review

Ask while the visit is still in mind: the same day or the next, on the ticket or in a message you already send. Do not hover for stars while they are still eating or in the chair.

Why the number of Google reviews matters, not only the stars

A listing with dozens of recent reviews and a 4.5 often persuades more than a 4.9 with eight opinions from 2019. Customers and Google both look at how alive the page is.

How to remove a Google review (and how to report a fake one)

Most genuine Google reviews cannot be deleted, even the ones that sting. This guide is for the owner who has just found a 1-star they did not expect, and wants the legal next step, not a miracle.

How to verify your Google Business Profile

If there is no Reply button on your reviews, the listing is probably not verified, or you are signed in with the wrong Google account. Verification is how you take ownership of the Google Business Profile (still searched as Google My Business) and start answering.

Google review response examples you can use this week

People look up “google review responses” when they have a blank box and two minutes. Here are replies you can adapt. Change the detail. Do not paste the same paragraph under every review.

How to respond to negative Google reviews

A 1-star or 2-star review is the one you want to answer, and the one that is easiest to get wrong. This is the sequence: wait, name the problem, take it off the listing, do not argue.

Do Google reviews help SEO and your Maps ranking?

Reviews are not a magic ranking switch. They are one of the things people, and Google, look at when they compare two shops on the same street. This piece is the honest version.
