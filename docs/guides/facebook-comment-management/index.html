Why BabyRock

BabyRock gives a Facebook Page one owner-supervised routine for public replies, recommendations and inbound DM awareness. Start free, then add paid inbound automation when you want faster private responses.

Give every Facebook Page comment a clear route

Facebook comments often sit under recommendations, shared posts and longer local discussions. A thread may include customers, neighbours and people comparing businesses, and it can stay visible for months.

Read the parent post and latest replies before answering. A useful public response helps the person asking while keeping private details out of public view.

Reply, hide or escalate with confidence

Reply to questions, thanks, recommendations and fair criticism. Hide spam, repeated links, abuse or comments that expose private information. Facebook Page controls handle hiding and deleting; BabyRock reviews and replies to public comments and escalates sensitive cases to the owner.

Escalate complaints, refunds, safety issues, legal threats, discrimination and pricing exceptions. The owner sees the comment and proposed response before publication. Delete only clear abuse or spam after saving evidence.

Scenario: a weekend recommendation thread stays calm

A customer recommends a plumbing business in a local group post. A neighbour asks for a weekend call-out price. Another person says a previous visit ran late.

Thank the recommender, answer the call-out question with the published range and invite a private message for the address. Escalate the lateness complaint. The owner checks the job and replies after the facts are clear. No private customer detail appears in the thread.

Keep public questions and recommendations on tone

Readers can see how a business handles disagreement. Keep replies short, name the situation plainly and avoid sarcasm. Thank recommendations, answer with facts and move detailed price or booking conversations to a message the customer starts.

BabyRock's brand voice settings help routine replies sound like the owner. Add local phrasing, formality and topics that must always wait for the owner.

Run Facebook comments and private messages from one owner rhythm

Check comments once in the morning and once later in the day. Clear routine replies, flag sensitive threads and note follow-ups. Then check DM alerts for people who contacted the business first.

BabyRock keeps public replies, sensitive escalation and DM alerts in one place. As the Page gets busier, paid social plans from 9.99 EUR per month add automatic replies to inbound DMs and more conversation capacity.

Questions buyers ask

What is the best way to manage Facebook comments?

Check comments in one scheduled routine, answer routine questions in the owner's voice, hide clear spam and escalate complaints or legal risks. Keep private details out of public replies.

Are comments on Facebook recommendations public?

Visible recommendation comments can be seen by other Facebook users. Keep addresses, order numbers and phone numbers in a private message. A short public reply can invite the customer to continue there.

Can I hide a comment from public view?

Page admins can use Facebook's own hide control to take a comment out of public view while preserving the original. BabyRock keeps public comment review and replies inside the owner's wider moderation routine.

Should I reply to every Facebook comment?

Reply to questions, recommendations and fair criticism. Leave clear spam out of the public conversation, escalate serious cases and let the owner decide when a reply would worsen the thread.

Does BabyRock cover Facebook comments on the free social workflow?

Yes. Facebook public replies sit alongside Instagram in the free social workflow, with DM alerts keeping inbound messages visible. Automatic replies to those DMs come with a paid social plan.

Do automated replies need owner approval?

Routine public replies can run in the approved brand voice, with staff attention focused on sensitive comments, complaints and exceptions that escalate to the owner.
