The monthly care that keeps your site accurate and supported

A working website needs more than an occasional update. Hosting needs oversight, security needs attention, backups need checking and small content requests accumulate. Website care groups those jobs into one monthly service with a single clear routine. The plan includes four small edits, hosting oversight, security, backups and a monthly report. It keeps a working site supported, while new or larger work moves into a separate build scope.

Hosting, security and backups handled in one clear service

Hosting oversight means BabyRock watches the site and acts on issues while the site stays with its existing host. Security covers the routine protection work needed to keep the site maintained. Backups provide a recoverable copy if an update or site issue causes a problem.

The monthly report makes the care routine visible. It records the work completed and the state of the site in plain language, with a direct approval step before content changes go live.

Use four small edits to keep hours, prices and people current

Small edits are practical changes that keep a site accurate between larger projects. Change hours, replace a staff photo, update a price, add a service description, swap a seasonal image or correct page text. Point to the existing page and exact content. The allowance is for focused updates within the current page structure. A new page, navigation, booking flow or redesign is build work.

Update clinic hours, staff and a service page in one request

A clinic changes its Friday hours, adds a new physiotherapist and updates the description of one existing service. The owner sends a screenshot of the current hours, the new times, a staff photo and bio, and the revised service text through WhatsApp.

Website care treats those as small edits because they replace content on existing pages. BabyRock prepares the changes, sends the preview for approval and publishes after the clinic confirms the details. The four edits fit inside one monthly allowance, and the monthly report records what changed.

Choose a steady care rhythm after launch

Care works after launch, when the website is live and the owner wants it to stay accurate without managing maintenance alone. The value is the routine: one place for small changes, a defined allowance and a report showing the site is looked after. It creates a clear next step after a build. Larger work remains separate.

Questions buyers ask

What is included in website care?

Website care includes hosting oversight, security, backups, four small content edits and a monthly report. Content changes are approved by the owner before publication.

How much does website care cost?

Website care costs 49.99 EUR a month. A supported website or rebuild assessment starts from 499 EUR once and is separate from the monthly service.

What counts as a small website edit?

A focused change to existing content, such as hours, prices, a photo, staff details or page text. A new page, navigation change, booking flow or redesign is build work.

How does website care relate to a new website?

Website care supports a working site. A new website or rebuild is scoped separately from 499 EUR once.

Can I cancel or change the plan?

Review the plan as the website's needs change and confirm the current billing terms with BabyRock before starting. The service is monthly.

Who approves website changes?

The owner approves content changes in WhatsApp. BabyRock prepares the proposed update and publishes it after confirmation.

Can website care manage several small changes at once?

Yes. Group related changes into one request with the page links, screenshots and replacement details. Each small edit uses part of the four-edit monthly allowance.
