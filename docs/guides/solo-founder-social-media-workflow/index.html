A social media workflow built for one founder

BabyRock gives a solo founder a social workflow that fits around client delivery: one planning block, a short daily triage and one WhatsApp channel for approvals. Paid inbound DM automation, Content 12 publishing and website care extend the routine as work grows.

Why BabyRock

Choose BabyRock when you want social media to run as a repeatable one-person system that protects client delivery time. Start with free replies and alerts, keep approvals in the owner channel and add paid help when the work earns it.

A daily social routine that protects your client time

The founder's schedule should fit around delivery work and protect focused time. Check comments and DM alerts twice: before the first client task and near day's end. Decisions move to one list; everything else waits for the weekly review.

This keeps the founder visible and responsive while leaving room for deep work. A quick public reply protects the customer experience; refunds, complaints and collaborations get proper attention later.

Batch content on Monday and triage in ten minutes

Monday, 45 minutes: choose three themes, draft captions and collect images or proof. If using paid Content 12, turn that into 12 publication briefs; otherwise prepare your own queue.

Each weekday, 10 minutes: answer public questions, scan DM alerts and flag sensitive items. Friday, 15 minutes: review the week and choose next Monday's themes.

Help a solo consultant protect client delivery days

A solo operations consultant keeps Tuesday and Thursday for client delivery. Morning triage takes ten minutes before the first call, and non-urgent notifications stay quiet during workshops. A post is approved from the owner WhatsApp channel between clients.

A client asks for a website hours change at the same time. The request goes through website care in the same owner thread. The consultant decides once, and the rest of the day remains protected.

Keep every approval and request in one WhatsApp thread

Use WhatsApp for owner decisions while customer conversations stay in Instagram, Facebook or email. The owner channel carries alerts, approvals and website requests with the context needed to act.

A useful approval message contains the asset, the decision required and the deadline. The founder replies yes, no or change this line. That keeps the decision attached to the work.

Add paid support when it creates more owner time

Free gives everyday public comment replies and DM alerts. Paid social starts at 9.99 EUR per month for 200 inbound conversations, or 24.99 EUR for 1,000. Content 12 is 9.99 EUR for 12 publications, Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR, and website care is 49.99 EUR.

Add paid help when unread messages delay customers, inbound replies need automation or publishing keeps slipping. Match each service to a clear job and keep the routine focused on the work that earns it.

Questions buyers ask

What is a realistic social media workflow for a solo founder?

Plan weekly, check comments and DMs twice daily, review loose ends Friday and keep approvals in one channel. The routine must fit around delivery work.

How often should I check comments and DMs?

Twice a day: before client work and near the end of the day. Use alerts for sensitive messages and leave routine replies for the next check.

Can I batch content without sounding repetitive?

Rotate themes such as the current offer, a real customer question and a point of view. Use different examples and images so the voice stays consistent without repeating sentences.

How do I approve posts while away from my desk?

Send the asset, decision required and deadline through the owner WhatsApp channel. Reply yes, no or one change so the decision stays attached to the work.

Which tasks should I delegate first?

Delegate public comment checks, content preparation and routine website edits first. Keep pricing exceptions, complaints, refunds, collaborations and final approvals with the founder.

Can website updates fit the same workflow?

Yes. Send the change through the owner channel. Optional website care at 49.99 EUR per month covers four small edits, security, backups, hosting oversight and a report.
