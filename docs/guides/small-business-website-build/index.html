Why BabyRock

Choose BabyRock when you want a credible website without a large agency engagement. Start with the assessment, then approve the page map and scope before work begins.

A focused website built around your customer's next step

The 499 EUR starting point covers the initial build assessment and the foundation of a focused business website. You explain what the business sells, who needs the site and what a visitor should do next. BabyRock turns that into a page map and written scope covering pages, content responsibilities and included build work.

Straightforward sites usually need a clear offer, proof, practical details and one obvious next step. Extra pages, complex functionality or substantial content work are quoted first.

Turn your real content and photos into a clear page map

Collect the current offer, prices or ranges, service area, opening hours, contact details, real photos and examples of work. Add the questions customers ask before they buy; those questions often become the strongest parts of the site.

The page map connects each page to a customer decision. A home page introduces the business, service pages answer specific questions, proof builds trust and a contact path makes the next step easy. The owner supplies accurate facts and timely approvals; BabyRock turns them into the page structure.

Help a new tutoring business launch a credible first site

A tutor leaving a larger centre wants a first website before taking on private students. The useful page map is home, subjects and levels, how lessons work, background and contact. The home page states subjects, age groups, location and teaching approach; the lesson page explains assessment, session structure and preparation.

The tutor supplies real qualifications, a clear portrait and genuine feedback. The next step is a conversation about the student's needs, supported by a simple enquiry route.

Review, approve and publish with one owner in control

The owner reviews the page map first, then the content and design in sequence. Approvals happen before the next stage depends on them. The WhatsApp project channel gives the owner one place for questions, confirmations and changes.

Before publishing, check the offer, names, contact details, images and links. Confirm that the main next step is easy to find on a phone. The final approval is the owner's.

Keep the new site current with optional website care

A website needs small changes as prices, hours, offers and team details change. Website care is an optional paid service at 49.99 EUR per month. It includes four small edits, security and backups, hosting oversight and a monthly report.

Care is separate from the one-off build. Owners can manage updates themselves, or send routine requests through the same workflow used for the project.

Questions buyers ask

How much does a small business website build cost?

A BabyRock website build starts at 499 EUR. The final price depends on the agreed pages, content and build work. Care is optional at 49.99 EUR per month.

What is included in the starting build price?

The starting point includes the build assessment, page map, written scope and build work within that scope. Extra pages, complex functionality or substantial content work are quoted first.

Can BabyRock rebuild an existing website?

Yes. The assessment reviews what the current site does well, what has changed and which content is worth keeping. Useful material is placed deliberately with owner review.

Do I need to write all the content?

You supply the business facts, offer details, photos and real proof. The build turns that material into the page structure for review. Substantial extra writing belongs in the agreed scope.

How does website care support the site after launch?

How is the final build scope agreed?

After the assessment, BabyRock sets out the page map, responsibilities, included work and optional extras. The owner approves that scope and price before the build proceeds.
