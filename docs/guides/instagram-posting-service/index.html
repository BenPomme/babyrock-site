Instagram posting service from photos to approved posts

BabyRock turns photos on your phone into a steady Instagram publishing rhythm. Send source material through WhatsApp, approve each post and let the paid content workflow handle the plan. Content 12 is 9.99 EUR a month for 12 publications; Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR for a heavier cadence.

Why BabyRock

Choose BabyRock Content 12 when 12 publications a month matches your cadence. Move to Content Unlimited for frequent posting. The workflow keeps the owner's approval at the centre.

Turn your camera roll into approved Instagram posts

Start with usable photos and a short note about what they show. A phone, natural light and real business detail provide enough source material. Paid content support prepares the post, including the caption and the next step for the audience.

The owner still decides what represents the business. Review the image, wording, service name, price and timing, then approve or request a change. Publication happens only after approval.

Use one weekly loop for photos, drafts and approval

Use the same rhythm each week. For example, send four to six photos and a few notes on Sunday, review proposed posts early in the week and approve final versions for their planned days.

A short note is enough: what the photo shows, who it is for and any fact that must be exact. The owner can reject an image, correct a caption or move an idea to another week.

Help a salon owner publish weekly from one Sunday handoff

A salon owner finishes the week with photos of a new colour, a product shelf and a stylist at work. On Sunday she sends the set through WhatsApp with the shade name, service and booking request customers should use.

The paid workflow proposes three posts for the coming week. She corrects one product name, holds a photo that needs customer permission and approves the other drafts for publication.

Send source material that makes every post stronger

Choose sharp photos in natural light and show the detail customers care about. Include the finished result as well as the process. Add the service name, location, audience and action customers should take.

Get permission before using a customer's face or a before-and-after image. Avoid screenshots with private messages, payment details or unrelated people in the background.

Choose Content 12 or Content Unlimited for your posting rhythm

Content 12 is 9.99 EUR per month for 12 publications. It suits an owner who wants roughly two or three posts each week and can submit photos once a week. Each post remains subject to owner approval.

Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR per month for a heavier cadence. Both paid options use Instagram publishing with WhatsApp submission and approval. The free plan keeps public comment replies and DM alerts running alongside them.

Questions buyers ask

What is an Instagram posting service?

It is a paid workflow that turns photos and notes supplied by the business into approved Instagram posts. The owner reviews each draft, and publication follows approval.

Can I send post photos through WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp is the owner command channel for content submission and approval. Send the photos with a short note about the service, audience and action.

Do I approve every post?

Yes. Owner approval is part of the workflow. You can approve the proposed post or ask for a caption, image or timing change before publication.

How many posts are included?

Content 12 includes 12 publications per month for 9.99 EUR. Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR per month for frequent publishing.

Can captions be refined before publishing?

Yes. Review the draft and request corrections to wording, service details, prices or the call to action. Only the approved version should publish.

How does Instagram posting fit with the free plan?

Instagram publishing is a paid Content 12 or Content Unlimited add-on. The free plan focuses on public comment replies and DM alerts.
