How Messenger automation fits a small business

Messenger is often the fastest way to ask about hours, availability, a repair or an appointment. Messages arrive while the owner is serving someone or closed for the evening. An automatic first reply keeps the conversation moving while the owner handles the exceptions.

BabyRock handles that inbound job. The owner approves routine replies, and sensitive cases stay with a person. The free plan includes public replies and DM alerts, while paid social from 9.99 EUR monthly adds automatic inbound replies and more conversation capacity.

Connect the Page and define approved reply paths

List the ten questions the Page receives most often and group them into paths such as hours, availability, appointments and order status. Write each approved answer in the owner's voice, then choose which situations must stop automation and alert the owner.

The Page connects through Meta. Prepare a routine question, a missing detail and a complaint as test paths, then refine the handoff before launch.

Scenario: a booking question arrives on a Saturday

A customer asks at 7:40 pm on Saturday for a table for six the next evening. The automatic reply confirms the request, asks for a preferred time and says the owner will confirm availability. The owner gets an alert with context.

The customer has supplied the key detail, so the owner can confirm availability or suggest another time. The customer gets a fast answer.

Keep orders, complaints and price exceptions with the owner

Refunds, damaged orders, delays, custom quotes and complaints need context and a responsible person. The Page should acknowledge the message, collect only the details needed and route it to the owner.

Any mention of a refund, order number, allergy, complaint or price exception can create an alert. The owner replies personally or asks for order details in a private message. Automation protects speed; the owner protects the decision.

Keep Messenger and Instagram replies consistent

Customers move between Instagram and Messenger without noticing. Use the same facts, greeting, reply boundaries and escalation rules on both. When hours or prices change, update both sets of replies together.

Routine questions should feel familiar on either channel, and unusual requests should reach the same decision maker. Review week one before adding paths.

Questions buyers ask

Can I automate Facebook Messenger replies?

Yes, on a paid plan after the Page connects. BabyRock answers approved inbound questions and escalates sensitive cases.

Is Messenger automation free?

Messenger automation is part of paid social. The free plan gives the owner public replies and DM alerts, while automatic replies start at 9.99 EUR per month for 200 conversations.

Who can receive an automatic reply?

People who message the Page first. BabyRock keeps every automatic reply tied to that inbound conversation and the owner's approved paths.

Can I approve replies before they send?

You approve reply paths and escalation rules before launch. Routine replies run, while complaints and exceptions go to the owner.

How does Page setup work?

BabyRock connects through Meta. Content and reply planning can begin during setup, and live Page replies start once the account connection is active.

How is Messenger automation priced?

Paid social starts at 9.99 EUR monthly for 200 conversations or 24.99 EUR for a larger allowance. The free plan includes DM alerts.
