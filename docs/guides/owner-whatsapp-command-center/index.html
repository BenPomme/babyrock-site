Why BabyRock

Choose BabyRock when approvals, sensitive replies and website changes compete for your attention. One owner thread keeps each decision visible and lets the rest of the work continue around it.

Bring every owner decision into one WhatsApp thread

An owner channel carries decisions only the owner can make: a low-rated review that needs a careful reply, a private message about a refund or safety issue, a post waiting for approval and a website edit that changes a price or opening hour.

Routine public questions stay in the social channel, private conversations stay where they started, and the owner receives the specific decision with context. That keeps the command center focused on action.

Start the day with reviews, comments, DMs and approvals

07:45, ten minutes: read the overnight summary, clear flagged comments, check DMs that need a decision, approve the day's post and send the website change.

The owner replies yes, no or change this. The team records the decision and closes the request. A complete morning pass beats a day watching notifications.

Keep a multi-site owner in control while travelling

A multi-site owner travels between locations most weeks. The team handles routine comment replies and prepares content, but the owner still approves an offer that mentions two branches and answers a complaint about delayed service.

The morning briefing arrives as one thread. The owner approves the post with a corrected time, asks the team to call the customer privately and sends a website hours update through care. The owner acts from WhatsApp with the full context in view.

Set clear escalation rules and protected focus time

Write escalation rules in plain language. Safety, legal, staff, refunds, public complaints and press inquiries go to the owner. Opening hours, directions, simple product questions and appointment availability follow the routine reply path.

Agree quiet hours with a named route for exceptions, so the owner can focus on the current task with confidence that urgent cases still reach the right person. Paid social from 9.99 EUR adds automatic inbound DM replies when faster private response is useful.

Keep customer conversations in their original channel

WhatsApp stays owner-facing. Automated replies go to people who contacted the business first, and sensitive decisions are escalated. That keeps every customer exchange in context while the owner handles the decisions that need a person.

Website requests and content approvals stay in the owner thread, while the customer conversation remains where it started. That protects privacy and keeps the command center focused on action.

Questions buyers ask

How does WhatsApp fit the customer workflow?

WhatsApp is the owner-facing command channel. Customer conversations stay in the social platform where they started, while the owner receives the decisions that need attention.

What can an owner approve from WhatsApp?

Sensitive public replies, content posts and website changes. Routine public replies and team work can continue without an owner decision.

Can I receive DM and comment alerts there?

Yes. The owner channel carries DM and comment attention with the context needed to decide what happens next.

Can I approve content posts from WhatsApp?

Yes. Posts are owner-approved, and publication can be handled through the paid Content 12 or Content Unlimited add-ons.

Can I request website updates over WhatsApp?

Yes. Send the exact change through the owner channel. Optional website care at 49.99 EUR per month covers four small edits, security, backups, hosting oversight and a report.

What does the owner handle in WhatsApp?

The owner handles sensitive replies, content approvals and website requests. Routine public replies and team work continue in the background, with the relevant context attached to each decision.
