Turn Instagram and Facebook comments into daily customer care

BabyRock gives a busy owner one calm queue for Instagram and Facebook comments: routine questions get a quick reply in your voice, sensitive threads reach you with context, and inbound DMs stay visible. Add paid inbound DM automation when you want the first private response handled automatically.

Why BabyRock

BabyRock turns scattered comment checking into one owner-led customer care rhythm. Start with free public replies and DM alerts, then add paid inbound DM automation when you want faster private conversations.

One shared comment queue for Instagram and Facebook

A shared queue turns two apps into one daily shift. The owner works from one list of public comments that need attention, so a question gets the same care wherever it arrived. BabyRock keeps Instagram and Facebook comment replies in one workflow.

Sort the queue into routine questions, recommendations, sensitive threads and sales enquiries. That structure stops every comment from feeling urgent.

A 20-minute morning triage that protects the rest of the day

Set a timer and work in the same order every morning, even on a busy day.

  • First, scan overnight comments for safety issues, complaints and direct questions.
  • Next, answer routine public questions such as opening hours, product availability or service areas.
  • Then, route sensitive comments and unusual requests to the owner with the context needed to decide.
  • Finish by checking DM alerts for people who have already contacted the business.

Give routine replies, sensitive threads and sales enquiries the right route

Routine comments need speed and a familiar tone. A simple public answer helps everyone with the same question. BabyRock can reply in the owner's voice while keeping control.

Sensitive comments need judgment. Complaints, legal threats, safety concerns and pricing exceptions should wait for the owner. Sales questions can get a helpful public answer and an invitation to send a private message. The customer starts the DM, so the private conversation follows a clear invitation.

Keep every public reply recognisably yours

Public replies are part of the visible voice. BabyRock's brand voice settings help routine answers sound like the owner, with natural greetings, formality and sign-offs. Set examples and mark topics that must always wait for the owner.

Review the examples after a busy week. A few approved habits create consistency while each reply fits the moment. A local shop and a creator brand each keep their own distinct tone.

Questions buyers ask

How do I manage comments on Instagram and Facebook in one workflow?

Use one daily queue for comments from both platforms. BabyRock supports eligible public replies, owner escalation and DM alerts in one social routine.

Should every public comment get the same reply?

Routine questions can share approved answers, while recommendations, complaints and sales messages need different wording. Keep the voice consistent and let the situation set the reply.

Which comments should wait for the owner?

Complaints, safety issues, legal threats, abuse, refunds and unusual pricing go to the owner before any public response.

Does comment automation sound robotic?

A generic template can sound robotic. BabyRock uses brand voice settings for routine public replies and escalates sensitive comments, so the owner defines the tone.

Is public comment management free for a small account?

The free plan includes public Instagram and Facebook replies plus DM alerts for a smaller Instagram account. Automatic DM replies and higher capacity are available on paid social plans.

When does a business need a paid social plan?

Choose paid when you want automatic replies to inbound DMs or more conversation capacity than the free workflow. Plans start at 9.99 EUR per month.

How does a public comment continue in a DM?

Reply publicly with the useful answer, then invite the customer to send a DM. Once they message first, paid inbound automation can handle the first response and bring exceptions to the owner.
