Instagram DM automation for a faster first reply

BabyRock answers people who message your Instagram first: routine questions get a clear reply in your voice, sensitive conversations reach you with context, and the owner can take over at any point. Start with free DM alerts, then add paid automatic replies when you want the inbox covered.

Why BabyRock

BabyRock turns inbound Instagram DMs into a calm, owner-supervised response flow. Free DM alerts keep new messages visible, and paid plans from 9.99 EUR per month add automatic replies for the conversations that need a fast first answer.

Give every inbound DM a strong first reply

BabyRock responds to conversations a customer starts, keeping automation tied to a real request. A public comment can invite someone to message, and the customer opens the DM when they are ready.

Paid automation begins when that inbound message arrives. The first reply acknowledges the question, uses approved facts and gives the customer a clear next step in the owner's voice.

Map the first three messages a customer may send

Design a short path. The first reply acknowledges the message and identifies the topic. The second gives the published answer, asks one useful question or offers a next step. The third closes the loop or hands over to a person.

Common paths include hours, availability, delivery, service coverage, published prices and reporting a problem. Discounts, refunds, safety issues and complaints leave the routine path and reach the owner.

Scenario: a product price question arrives after hours

At 21:40, someone messages a homeware shop: How much is the starter set? The shop has a published starting price but needs colour and postcode before confirming an order.

The reply shares the price and asks for both details, keeping to published facts and approved availability. A refund request or damaged-order message escalates to the owner. In the morning, the owner sees the alert and replies personally.

Keep sensitive conversations with the owner

Set handoff rules before switching on replies. The owner should receive complaints, legal issues, refunds, safety concerns, custom quotes and partnership requests. Include the customer's message and proposed next step.

Brand voice settings keep routine replies in the owner's tone, while sensitive cases wait for a person. The owner can review and take over any conversation that needs judgment.

Build a response rhythm that earns trust

Answer promptly within supported hours, state when a person is available and keep non-urgent replies inside reasonable quiet hours. A clear next step helps the customer move forward with confidence.

Questions buyers ask

How does Instagram DM automation work?

It gives people who message the business first a fast, approved reply. Common questions move forward automatically, while sensitive cases escalate to the owner.

Are automated Instagram DMs included in the free plan?

Free DM alerts keep new messages visible. Automatic inbound replies are part of paid social and use the plan's conversation allowance.

Which conversations can receive an automatic reply?

Conversations the customer starts. Every automatic response stays tied to that inbound request and follows the approved reply path.

How can a public comment continue in a DM?

Invite the customer to message you from the public reply. Automatic handling begins after the customer sends that DM.

Can an owner review sensitive DM replies?

Yes. Complaints, refunds, safety issues, custom quotes and legal questions escalate. The owner can review and take over before a sensitive response.

How much does DM automation cost?

Paid social starts at 9.99 EUR per month for 200 conversations, with the Grow plan at 24.99 EUR for a larger allowance. The free plan includes DM alerts.

What happens when a message needs a person?

The conversation escalates with the customer's message and suggested next step. The owner answers personally or updates the approved information.
