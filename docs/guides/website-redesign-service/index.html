Why BabyRock

Choose BabyRock when the business has changed faster than the website. The assessment protects useful content, rebuilds the customer path and settles what should change before the new site is built.

Give an outdated site a clearer story and an easier customer path

The clearest sign is a site that makes customers work to understand the current business. The home page may lead with a service you no longer sell, the proof may be years old and the contact step may be buried on a phone. If the owner avoids sending the link, the site has outgrown the business.

A redesign is due when the offer, audience or customer path has changed enough that a larger change now serves the story better.

Audit useful content before the rebuild starts

Start with an inventory of what the site already has. List every page, URL, useful paragraph, photo, proof point and enquiry route. Mark each as keep, rewrite or retire, then let the owner choose what still represents the business.

Your domain stays yours, and useful content is reviewed and placed deliberately. Each page earns its place in the new structure, so the rebuild starts with the strongest material and a clear purpose.

Bring an old site in line with today's offer

A consultancy's 2019 site still describes hourly workshops and hides the current advisory package. The new home page leads with the present offer, a client situation, the working process and one enquiry step. The old workshop page either retires or becomes a focused service page if it still earns enquiries.

The owner keeps a useful case example and rewrites the biography. The rebuild makes a deliberate choice for every page.

Rebuild around the customer's decision path

Build the page sequence around how buyers decide. A home page names the problem and offer, service pages answer scope questions, proof shows real work and the contact step explains what happens next. Each page should have one job and one obvious next action.

The new site should match the sales conversation the owner already has. That matters more than a long feature list.

Choose a care routine that keeps the new site current

After launch, decide who can change prices, hours, team details and offers. Website care is optional at 49.99 EUR per month, with four small edits, security and backups, hosting oversight and a monthly report.

Care is separate from the one-off rebuild. A simple monthly routine keeps small changes current and gives the owner a clear route for updates.

Questions buyers ask

When should a small business redesign its website?

Redesign when the site no longer explains the current offer, hides the next step or makes the business look less capable than it is. The decisive question is whether the site still supports the business customers meet today.

Can I keep my domain and existing content?

Your domain stays yours. Useful copy, photos and proof can be reused after review, and weak or outdated material can be retired.

Is a redesign different from a one-off build?

It uses the same 499 EUR starting point but begins with an audit of the existing site, while a first build starts from a new page map.

How are old pages handled in a redesign?

Pages are reviewed and placed deliberately. Useful content is reused, weak material is retired and every page in the new site has a clear job.

What happens to search visibility during a redesign?

Visibility can change when pages move. The assessment identifies valuable URLs so the owner can decide what to keep and where redirects support customers and search visibility.

How does monthly website care fit a redesign?

Website care is optional at 49.99 EUR per month, with four small edits, security and backups, hosting oversight and a monthly report.
