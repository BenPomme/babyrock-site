One social media system for creators and influencers

BabyRock gives creators one owner-led system for consistent content, warm public replies and inbound DMs, so fan conversation and collaboration inquiries keep moving without taking over creative time. Paid inbound DM automation and content publication are ready when volume grows.

Why BabyRock

Choose BabyRock when the account has outgrown constant app checking while the creator keeps control of brand deals and sensitive messages. Start with free replies and alerts, then add paid automation and publishing as the work grows.

Run content, community and collaborations in one creator week

A creator's week brings planning, shooting, editing, publishing, public replies, inbound DMs and brand opportunities into one busy rhythm.

A useful system gives each job a time box: one planning session, a short daily community check, one collaboration review and a weekly look at what to publish next.

Keep fan replies warm as the audience grows

Public comments are where new followers decide whether the creator is approachable. Answer questions, acknowledge shared experiences and point people to the right resource. Keep the creator's phrasing, including the words fans recognise.

DM alerts surface people who contacted the account first. Paid social can automate replies to those inbound messages, while brand deals, criticism and personal disclosures go to the creator.

Help a travel creator catch up after a shoot

A travel creator returns from a three-day shoot to hundreds of comments and a crowded inbox. Before the trip, the next publications are planned and the creator writes short replies for recurring questions about gear, locations and collaborations.

After landing, the creator checks a morning summary, approves the next post and reviews flagged messages. Routine fan replies continue in the creator's voice; a hotel partnership, a safety concern and a paid usage request stay with the creator.

Give collaboration DMs their own clear owner path

Treat collaboration mail as a small pipeline with its own commercial details. A useful message names the brand, campaign, deliverable, fee or budget range, usage rights and deadline. The creator decides which opportunities deserve a reply and which need a polite decline.

Keep audience appreciation separate from commercial negotiation. A clear flag and an owner decision keep the workflow focused on the conversation and the next step.

Choose the paid support that matches your creative rhythm

The free plan gives everyday public comment replies and DM alerts. Paid social begins at 9.99 EUR per month for 200 inbound conversations, with the Grow plan at 24.99 EUR for 1,000. Automated replies stay inside conversations that people started with the creator.

Content 12 is an optional paid add-on at 9.99 EUR per month for 12 publications; Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR. Add them when the creator wants inbound automation or a reliable publication cadence.

Questions buyers ask

How can creators manage their social media alone?

Use one weekly planning block, a short daily comment and DM check, and a separate review for collaborations. Batch the repeatable work and keep sensitive decisions for the creator.

Does BabyRock support influencer accounts?

Yes, through public comment replies, DM alerts and paid inbound DM automation for a creator account, with the creator keeping decisions on collaborations and sensitive messages.

How are fan comments and collaboration DMs different?

Fan comments are public community conversation. Collaboration DMs need commercial details and the creator's decision. Keep the two queues separate so brand requests follow their own owner-led path.

How do automated DMs work for creators?

They stay inside conversations people start with the creator. Sensitive and commercial decisions go to the creator, and the workflow keeps every reply tied to that inbound thread.

Can I publish posts through a monthly content service?

Yes. Content 12 is a paid add-on for 12 publications per month at 9.99 EUR, and Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR. The creator still approves the posts.

When does a creator need a paid plan?

Add paid social when the creator wants automatic replies to inbound DMs, more conversation capacity or a managed publishing cadence. Paid social starts at 9.99 EUR per month.
