Run the customer conversation from one owner WhatsApp thread

BabyRock turns WhatsApp into the owner's control line for the work customers can see: Instagram and Facebook comments, paid inbound DMs, Google reviews, content directions and website updates. The owner gets the decisions that need judgment, while routine replies run in the brand voice. A free entry covers everyday Google and social engagement, with paid options for inbound DM automation, content and website care. WhatsApp Business and specialist messaging platforms also have real strengths, and the right choice depends on the job the business needs finished.

Why BabyRock

BabyRock is the stronger choice when an owner wants public comments, paid inbound DMs, reviews, content and website work coordinated from one WhatsApp thread. AiSensy, Wati and respond.io suit customer messaging, campaigns or shared inboxes, and WhatsApp Business offers a capable native starting point.

Start with the job the owner needs finished

A small business can run a useful WhatsApp presence with native tools: a catalog, quick replies, labels and saved answers. BabyRock adds the wider public workflow when comments and inbound DMs spread across Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp. Public replies carry the brand voice, important DMs reach the owner, paid plans automate routine inbound answers, and review drafts, summaries and listing updates stay in one thread.

That structure suits an owner who wants to stay close to the customer relationship while the business grows. It also leaves room for a specialist platform where a team needs high-volume customer support, structured campaigns or a dedicated shared inbox.

Turn a public comment into a private conversation

A public comment deserves a public answer. BabyRock replies in the brand voice with the context of the post, then can invite the person to continue privately when the question needs order details, a quote or booking information. The next step begins when that person messages the business: paid plans answer routine inbound questions automatically and bring complaints, negotiations and unusual requests to the owner.

Start is 9.99 EUR a month for a smaller inbound community, and the Grow plan is 24.99 EUR a month for a busier account. A creator can keep a launch conversation warm, a salon can catch booking interest and a local shop can answer repeated product questions in a personal tone.

Questions buyers ask

What can BabyRock do with WhatsApp?

BabyRock gives the owner one WhatsApp thread for alerts, sensitive replies, summaries, content directions and website requests. It handles Instagram and Facebook public comments, inbound DMs and Google reviews in the brand voice, with paid plans adding automated inbound DM replies.

How do comments lead to a DM?

BabyRock can answer the public comment in the brand voice and invite the person to continue privately. The next step begins when that person contacts the business, where paid plans handle routine inbound questions and escalate sensitive decisions to the owner.

Which parts of BabyRock are paid?

BabyRock has a free entry for everyday Google and social engagement. Automated inbound DM replies use Start at 9.99 EUR a month or the Grow plan at 24.99 EUR a month. Content 12 is 9.99 EUR, Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR, website care is 49.99 EUR and a supported build or rebuild starts from 499 EUR once.

Can WhatsApp Business cover this on its own?

WhatsApp Business is a strong free starting point for a catalog, quick replies, labels and direct customer chat. BabyRock adds public social comments, paid inbound DM handling, Google review judgment, content and website coordination.

How are sensitive Google reviews handled?

One to three star reviews start as an owner draft, so the owner can approve the reply or pause it. Four and five star praise can move automatically, and sensitive cases stay with the owner's judgment.

How should a small business choose?

WhatsApp Business provides a capable native setup for a catalog, quick replies, labels and direct chat.
