Why BabyRock

Choose BabyRock to turn scattered social messages into one accountable customer care rhythm. Start with free public replies and DM alerts, then add paid inbound automation as conversations grow.

Build a response matrix that makes every customer message easier to answer

List the messages the business receives most often: hours, availability, order status, product help, complaints and urgent issues. For each, write the first response, choose public or private and mark whether the owner must decide.

A shared table with five columns is enough: message type, first reply, public or private, owner needed and next step. BabyRock supports the repeatable part through public replies, DM alerts and paid inbound automation.

Give pre-sale, order and complaint conversations the right route

Pre-sale questions usually belong in public when the answer helps other customers. Order questions need a private thread with references, addresses or delivery details. Complaints need both a short public acknowledgement and a private place to resolve.

Keep a clear public answer wherever it builds trust and helps other customers. Move to private when personal information or a sensitive decision is involved.

Resolve a delivery delay without losing the customer thread

A customer comments under a post asking where an order is. The public reply apologises and asks for the order reference by DM. In the private thread, the customer supplies it and the owner receives an alert.

The owner checks delivery status and replies with facts and a realistic update. The public comment can receive a short follow-up when resolved, without exposing order details. The customer gets one consistent answer.

Hand over difficult conversations with the context to help

A handoff should include the channel, customer name, issue, order reference, what was said and the next promised step. The owner can help without asking the customer to repeat everything.

Free DM alerts keep the owner aware. Paid plans from 9.99 EUR monthly for 200 conversations add automatic replies to inbound messages, with 24.99 EUR covering 1,000. Complaints and exceptions remain owner decisions. WhatsApp keeps the owner channel focused on decisions while customer conversations stay where they started.

Turn one week of conversations into a faster service rhythm

At the end of the week, review which questions repeated, which messages waited and which conversations needed the owner. Update the public answers and escalation rules.

Track categories, unanswered messages, repeat questions, owner handoffs and whether each issue reached a clear next step. A quieter inbox and fewer repeated explanations are useful signals of a stronger routine.

Questions buyers ask

What is social media customer service?

It is the routine and exceptional help a business gives through comments and DMs. A good workflow answers simple questions and sends sensitive cases to a person.

How fast should a small business reply on social media?

Set a standard you can keep. Aim for one business day on routine questions, with faster internal escalation for delivery or safety issues, and show the expected timing in the reply.

Which messages need a human?

Refunds, order problems, complaints, safety concerns, custom quotes and anything involving an exception should reach the owner.

Can comments and DMs share one escalation path?

Yes at workflow level. Keep public replies public, move personal details to a DM and send both paths to the same owner with context.

What role does WhatsApp play in customer care?

BabyRock uses WhatsApp as the owner channel for alerts and decisions. Customer conversations stay in Instagram, Facebook or email so each thread remains in the right place.

Which service signals should we review each week?

Track repeat questions, unanswered messages, owner handoffs and whether each issue reached a clear next step.
