Why BabyRock

For a site built, cared for and updated without another agency meeting on your calendar, BabyRock is the pocket web agency for the job. Send the project details or the change on WhatsApp, approve the result, and get back to your customers.

One pocket team for your build, care and small updates

Pocket means one focused team: one request channel, clear paid scopes and direct approval. One team covers three jobs. A build creates or rebuilds the site. Care keeps a working site supported each month. An update handles a focused change such as hours, prices, photos or page text. Choose the starting point that matches the job.

Send every website request through one familiar channel

Send the page link, screenshot and exact change through WhatsApp. For a build, send business details, pages needed and brand assets. For an update, point to the sentence, price, image or hours that should change, then add the replacement. BabyRock reviews the request, prepares the change and sends a preview or summary for approval. You confirm before publication, keeping request, decision and result in one conversation.

Add a service page, winter hours and an offer in one workflow

A cleaning business wants a new service page, winter hours and a seasonal offer. The new page is build work from 499 EUR once after scope and content are agreed. Hours and offer are small edits within the existing structure. With care, those two changes use two of four monthly edits. The owner sends old and new hours, offer wording and a photo. BabyRock prepares the changes, the owner approves and they go live. A one-off update can also be scoped as a paid request.

Pick the paid scope that moves your website forward

Start with the outcome you need. Build is for a supported website or rebuild assessment, with scope agreed first. Care is for an existing site that needs a monthly routine and edit allowance. Update is for one focused change within the current page structure. The scopes connect: a build can be followed by care, and care absorbs routine changes, all through one supplier.

Approve website changes without another agency meeting

A phone number change can move through a short approval loop. You see the proposed change, check the detail and approve it in WhatsApp. Larger build decisions get a clear scope before work starts. The owner stays in control of words, images and offers published under the business name. Care adds a monthly report so maintenance and edits stay visible.

Questions buyers ask

What does web agency in your pocket mean?

It means one compact service for website build, care and small updates, with requests and owner approvals handled through one WhatsApp thread and one accountable team.

Can BabyRock build my website?

Yes. A supported website or rebuild assessment starts from 499 EUR once. The pages, content and scope are agreed before the build begins.

Can BabyRock maintain an existing website?

Yes, when the site is supported. Website care costs 49.99 EUR a month and includes hosting oversight, security, backups, four small edits and a monthly report.

How do I request a small update?

Send the page link, a screenshot and the exact replacement through WhatsApp. BabyRock confirms the scope, prepares the change and sends it for approval.

Do I keep ownership of my domain and content?

You keep ownership of your domain, content and brand assets. Record who holds the registrar and hosting accounts before work starts.

Are website services included in the social plans?

Website build, care and updates are paid services with their own scopes. Free social support focuses on public comment replies and DM alerts, while website work is added separately.
