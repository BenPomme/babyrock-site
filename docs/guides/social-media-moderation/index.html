Social media moderation that keeps the owner in control

BabyRock gives a small team a calm moderation routine: public comments receive a reply in the business voice, routine questions move quickly, and threats, complaints or private details reach the owner with context. The owner keeps every difficult decision.

Why BabyRock

BabyRock makes public moderation easier to run without loosening owner control. Start with free public replies and DM alerts, then add paid inbound DM automation when private conversations need faster coverage.

Set clear house rules that make fast decisions easy

State what the business welcomes, what it will not allow, who can speak for the brand and which topics reach the owner. Cover spam, personal data, threats, discrimination, misleading claims and repeated abuse.

Decide where to reply, hide, delete or escalate. Name the evidence to keep and the owner's contact route. The policy should guide judgment under pressure.

Reply, hide, delete or escalate with a repeatable routine

Reply to questions, fair criticism, misunderstandings and recommendations. Hide comments that break the rules but need not be removed. Delete only clear spam or abuse after saving evidence. Facebook and Instagram controls remain with the owner.

Escalate threats, safety concerns, legal issues, discrimination and refund disputes. BabyRock reviews and replies to public comments and sends sensitive cases to the owner, who decides every legal or safety question.

Scenario: a tense weekend thread reaches the right person

A local studio posts a new class schedule. One commenter says a booking was cancelled without warning. Two others join with accusations. The thread becomes tense on Saturday night.

Save the original comment, timestamps and booking references. Hide threats or personal data, leave fair criticism visible and do not argue after hours. BabyRock flags the thread to the owner. On Monday, the owner checks the facts and posts one short reply that acknowledges the concern and invites the customer to message. The customer starts that DM.

Keep public replies calm and the decision record clear

Keep a short log: date, platform, comment link, rule, action, decision maker and evidence. The log makes repeat decisions easier and protects the business if a complaint escalates.

Keep public replies brief: acknowledge the concern, say what happens next and move sensitive details to a private conversation. Do not quote private messages or continue a back and forth. BabyRock's brand voice settings keep routine replies consistent, while owner escalation protects hard calls.

Turn moderation into a reliable monthly routine

Walk through a complaint, spam link, medical claim and threat before the team handles a live thread. Correct close calls and record the decision so the next shift can act with confidence.

The free plan gives the owner a practical starting point for public replies and DM alerts. As volume grows, paid social plans from 9.99 EUR per month add automatic replies to inbound DMs and more conversation capacity. Sensitive cases continue to route to the owner.

Questions buyers ask

What should a social media moderation policy include?

Include allowed and prohibited comments, reply and escalation rules, who can hide or delete, evidence to keep and the owner's approval route. Keep it short enough to use.

When should a business hide or delete a comment?

Hide spam, abuse or private information when it breaks the rules. Delete only clear spam or serious abuse after saving evidence. Let the owner decide unclear cases.

Should we reply publicly to every negative comment?

Use a calm public acknowledgement for fair criticism and misunderstandings. Route threats, legal issues and private disputes to the owner. A short, clear response works better than a long argument.

How do we escalate abuse and preserve the evidence?

Save the comment, author, timestamp, post link and relevant order details before hiding or deleting. Send the owner a short summary and proposed next step.

Can moderation be automated?

Routine public replies can follow approved rules. BabyRock supports public review and replies and escalates sensitive comments. Hiding and deleting remain platform and owner decisions.

Does moderation guarantee that a comment will be removed?

Use platform reporting and keep a record when a post sits outside the business's own controls. The owner reviews the available platform options and decides the next step.
