One social media routine for your small business

BabyRock brings your small business social work into one owner-led routine: plan posts, answer public comments, notice inbound DMs, handle reviews and route website requests without losing the thread. One owner-led system for your whole social week

A small business runs best with one operating system: a weekly plan for posts, a daily check of public comments and inbound DMs, a review routine, a place for website requests and one owner for risky decisions.

BabyRock gives that system a common home. Routine public replies can run in brand voice, DM alerts surface people who contacted the business first, and sensitive messages or posts wait for owner approval.

Plan Monday, answer daily and review on Friday

Monday, 30 minutes: choose three themes from the offer, recent customer questions and one piece of real proof. Draft the posts and note what needs a photo or owner quote.

Every morning, 10 minutes: answer public questions, read DM alerts, flag refunds, complaints or safety issues and note website changes. Friday, 20 minutes: review unresolved conversations and set the next week.

Help a two-person studio share the work without losing the voice

A two-person pilates studio divides the work without dividing the voice. The owner approves posts and handles sensitive replies about injuries, refunds or staff. The front-desk teammate checks public comments each morning, answers opening-hours questions and flags any private message that needs the owner.

One shared plan keeps class questions moving without waiting for the owner's next desk day. Public answers stay prompt while private decisions remain accountable.

Add the right level of paid support as the work grows

Free gives everyday public comment replies, DM alerts and eligible Google review replies. Automatic replies to inbound DMs start with paid social at 9.99 EUR per month for 200 conversations, or 24.99 EUR for 1,000. Automated replies go to people who contacted the business first.

Content 12 is an optional paid add-on at 9.99 EUR per month for 12 publications, Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR, and website care is 49.99 EUR. Each add-on extends the routine with a clear, focused scope.

Delegate confidently while the owner keeps the decisions

Delegate checking comments, preparing weekly plans and routing routine website requests. Keep pricing exceptions, refunds, complaints, safety issues, collaborations and final post approval with the owner.

Set a short voice guide with phrases the business uses and topics it avoids. Routine replies follow that guide, automated responses sound like the business, and sensitive cases go to the owner. Every automated reply stays inside a conversation the customer started.

Questions buyers ask

What does social media management include for a small business?

Planning, public replies, DM alerts, review attention and a route for website requests. Paid social adds inbound DM replies; publishing and website care are paid add-ons.

Can BabyRock handle comments, DMs and posts together?

Yes. Public comment replies and DM alerts share one workflow. Inbound DM automation is paid, and posts use the paid content add-ons.

Which parts are free and which are paid?

Free includes public replies, DM alerts and eligible Google review replies. Paid social starts at 9.99 EUR per month. Content and website care are separate paid services.

Do I need a separate website care plan?

Social and website care are separate services. Care is optional at 49.99 EUR per month for routine edits and maintenance.

How does WhatsApp support the team?

WhatsApp is the owner-facing coordination channel. Alerts, decisions and website requests stay there while customer conversations remain in their original social channels.

What is a simple way to start?

Start with three voice rules, a list of sensitive topics and a week of post themes. Deeper setup can follow as the routine settles.
