What a pocket community manager handles each week

The weekly job is simple: notice public questions, answer routine comments, review inbound DMs, prepare next posts and bring sensitive decisions to the owner. The value comes from doing those in the same order each week.

BabyRock keeps Instagram and Facebook public replies and DM alerts in one workflow. The paid Content 12 add-on adds 12 publications per month. The owner remains the decision maker.

Build a Monday-to-Friday rhythm for comments and DMs

Give each weekday one clear community job. A short rhythm is easier to keep than a long checklist.

  • Monday: clear weekend comments and DM alerts.
  • Tuesday: answer routine comments and escalate complaints.
  • Wednesday: review inbound DMs and flag owner decisions.
  • Thursday: approve the next content and check tone.
  • Friday: clear remaining comments and note one improvement.

Scenario: a cafe owner protects an hour for community care

A cafe owner has one hour before opening. Fifteen minutes go to overnight comments, ten to DM alerts and the rest to the next post. On Friday, a comment about a long wait arrives under a photo.

The routine drafts a short apology in the owner's voice and escalates it because it is a complaint. A question about opening hours gets a routine public reply. The difficult comment stays with the owner.

Keep every important decision with the owner

The owner sets the voice, approves content and handles sensitive cases. Complaints, refunds, safety issues and legal questions escalate with the comment and a suggested next step. Routine replies can follow the approved voice without a staff member checking every one.

Paid DM automation responds to people who message first, while the owner can review sensitive replies and take over at any point.

Choose the level of support that fits your week

Start free with public replies and DM alerts for a small account. As the week fills, paid social plans bring automatic replies to inbound DMs and more conversation capacity, while Content 12 adds 12 owner-approved publications a month.

Paid social starts at 9.99 EUR per month for 200 conversations, and the Grow plan is 24.99 EUR for a larger allowance. Content 12 is a separate paid add-on at 9.99 EUR per month. BabyRock gives the owner a supported weekly rhythm without the cost of a full-time community team.

Questions buyers ask

What is a community manager in your pocket?

It is a compact, owner-supervised routine for public comments, inbound DMs and content planning. BabyRock handles the repeatable daily work while the owner keeps brand and risk decisions.

Does it replace a full-time community manager?

It gives a small owner a reliable weekly layer for routine work. A larger brand with campaigns, partnerships and daily live coverage may still need a dedicated team.

Which community tasks can be handled each week?

Routine public replies, DM alerts, sensitive escalation, content approval and a paid 12-publication batch. The owner keeps brand and risk decisions.

How are sensitive replies approved?

Complaints, legal issues, refunds and safety concerns escalate to the owner with context. Routine replies follow the approved voice, while the owner stays available for the calls that matter.

Can the workflow include posts and DMs?

Yes. Content 12 adds 12 publications per month. The free plan gives the owner public replies and DM alerts as a starting point; automatic replies to inbound DMs come with paid social.

What does it cost to start?

Join for free to start with public replies and DM alerts. Paid social and Content 12 each start at 9.99 EUR per month.
