Questions buyers ask

What does the free plan include for Instagram?

The free plan includes public Instagram and Facebook comment replies plus DM alerts. Paid Start and the Grow plan add automated inbound DM replies, while content and website care are optional paid services. Manual platform replies remain a free native option.

How does BabyRock handle comment-to-DM conversations?

A verified public reply answers the comment in the brand voice and can invite the person to continue in an inbound DM. If the customer starts that message, paid Start or the Grow plan handle the reply, with sensitive decisions escalated to the owner. The customer chooses to send the DM after the public reply.

How does the free plan support a growing Instagram account?

The free plan covers public Instagram and Facebook comment replies plus DM alerts for accounts up to 1,000 Instagram followers. When the account passes that size, the owner has 30 days to choose a paid plan, while Google review replies remain free.

What does BabyRock need for Instagram comment replies?

A connected Instagram account is the starting point. Google review replies work separately with an eligible Google Business Profile.

Can BabyRock keep a consistent tone across replies?

Yes. Replies use the voice configured for the account, and routine public comments can run automatically while sensitive threads go to the owner.

Can BabyRock prepare and publish posts?

Yes. Content 12 is 9.99 EUR a month for 12 publications, while Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR. The owner approves the posts.

How is BabyRock different from Buffer or Manychat?

BabyRock brings public comment replies, owner escalation and DM alerts into one owner-friendly workflow. Buffer centers on publishing and comment management, while Manychat centers on flow-based social automation. Paid BabyRock plans extend the same workflow into inbound DM replies, content and website care.
