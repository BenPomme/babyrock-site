A social media content calendar built from your real business

BabyRock helps a small business turn services, busy periods and customer questions into a four-week Instagram and Facebook calendar, with owner approval built in. Add Content 12 from 9.99 EUR a month for 12 planned publications, or Content Unlimited at 29.99 EUR for a heavier publishing rhythm.

Why BabyRock

Choose BabyRock to replace last-minute posting with a clear monthly plan. Start with your own calendar, then add paid Content 12 or Content Unlimited when you want the publishing handled too.

Turn real business moments into a month of useful posts

List the moments already on the calendar: a new menu, seasonal hours, a service to explain and common customer questions. Add the photos you already have and the days available for review.

Group them into three useful jobs: help customers, show the work and invite a next step. A repair shop can explain a common fault, show a finished repair and invite people to ask for a collection slot.

Build a four-week plan in one focused owner session

Create one row per planned post with the week, topic, photo source, caption idea, owner and status. Pick a cadence you can sustain; two posts a week is one practical starting point.

Schedule time-sensitive material first, then alternate helpful and visible work. Leave one flexible slot for a late request. Finish with each row marked ready to draft, needs a photo or waiting for approval.

Keep a bakery visible through a quiet January

After the holiday rush, a bakery owner wants to stay visible without a large campaign. The January calendar has two posts each week: winter hours, a rye loaf from the Tuesday bake, a weekend cake-order reminder and a preview of a February class.

The owner chooses photos on Tuesday and reviews captions on Thursday. Each row records what the photo shows and the action customers should take. If a post changes order instructions, the owner checks that detail before approval.

Batch photos, captions and approvals in one clear pass

Collect photos in one folder and name them by week. Add a short note with the product, place and facts to check, then prepare captions without guessing what appears in the picture.

Review the month in one pass, with a final check close to publication. The owner confirms prices, dates and claims. Reuse evergreen posts only while their information remains correct.

Turn your calendar into a handled publishing rhythm

Submit photos and notes through WhatsApp, then approve each proposed post before it publishes. Instagram and Facebook publishing are handled through the paid add-on.

Questions buyers ask

How do I plan a month of social media content?

List business moments, customer questions and available photos. Give each post a row with its image, caption note and approval time.

How many posts should a small business publish each week?

Pick a cadence you can prepare and review consistently. Two posts per week is a practical starting point for many small businesses.

What belongs in an Instagram and Facebook calendar?

Include the week, topic, image source, caption note, owner, approval status and intended action. Keep prices and dates visible for a final check.

Can BabyRock help publish from a content calendar?

Yes, through a paid add-on. Content 12 covers 12 publications per month for 9.99 EUR, and Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR. The free plan focuses on public comment replies and DM alerts.

What does the Content 12 add-on include?

Content 12 covers 12 Instagram or Facebook publications monthly for 9.99 EUR. Submit photos and notes through WhatsApp, and approve each post before publication.

Can I approve posts before they publish?

Yes. Confirm the image, caption, price and time-sensitive details before a post goes live.
