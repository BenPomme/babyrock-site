A portfolio should show how you think, not just what you build

Prospective clients want to understand the constraint, the decision and the result. A considered Instagram presence and a clear website case study tell that story before the first call. BabyRock turns the material you already have into a coherent communication routine, with your judgement, credits and approval at the centre.

From completed project to approved case study and Instagram post

Start with one finished project. Send project photos and short notes through WhatsApp: the brief, site constraint, materials, key decisions, collaborators and preferred credit. Confirm what can be published. BabyRock prepares a supported website case study and image posts for Instagram and Facebook. You check the facts, drawings, names and tone, then approve. The case study publishes through the supported connection. Cross-posting the same item to Instagram and Facebook counts as one content item.

What the free plan and the Grow plan include

The free plan includes 500 public reply threads a month, 50 automated private conversations for approved FAQs and links, important DMs surfaced, 3 approved Instagram or Facebook image posts a month, and a weekly factual insight.

The Grow plan costs €39.99/month including VAT and includes 5,000 public reply threads, 300 automated private conversations with structured qualification, 12 approved image posts a month, request capture with owner reminders and response tracking, a weekly plan and next-post draft, and owner WhatsApp takeover.

Launch terms are planned for the offer described here. Access opens at launch.

Project enquiries arrive ready for a considered reply

The Grow plan asks for the project type, location, stage, timing, relevant budget range and contact details. The enquiry stays attached to one case, with owner reminders and response tracking. You decide the next step. No meeting or confirmed project is created until the practice confirms it. Important DMs surface in the same owner workflow.

Your authorship, credits and approvals stay intact

The practice supplies project photos and notes, confirms permission and chooses the credit line. BabyRock does not invent projects, clients or outcomes. A project name, address, client detail or collaborator credit is published only after owner approval. The Grow plan covers one approved case study a month on a supported connection. A new website, redesign, technical work or ongoing care is separately scoped and priced.

A weekly rhythm that protects design time

Use a simple four-step routine. Choose the project or idea that deserves attention this week. Send the source material in one WhatsApp thread. Review the draft against the facts and credits. Approve, publish and note the next action. The free plan includes a weekly factual insight and one suggestion. The Grow plan adds a weekly plan, next-post draft or brief, reminders and a review of results.

Three illustrative portfolio moments

Enquiry example: a Ghent townhouse owner has planning permission for a rear extension, wants to start interviews in September and gives a 180,000 EUR build budget. The details reach the architect for a personal reply.

Case study example: six timber-framed studio photos, one plan drawing and a note on the planning constraint. The architect approves the drawings and credit before publication on a supported website connection.

Instagram example: a close detail of a stone threshold with a 70-word note on material choice. The practice approves the image and wording before publication. These are workflow examples, not results.

Questions owners ask

Can BabyRock turn a completed project into a website case study?

Yes, on the Grow plan. One approved case study a month can be prepared from real project photos and notes and published on a supported website connection.

How do project photos and notes become a post or a case study?

You send the material through WhatsApp. BabyRock prepares the draft, you check the facts, drawings and credits, and you approve it. Publishing uses the supported connected channels.

Which details arrive with a project enquiry?

The Grow plan requests the project type, location, stage, timing, budget range and contact details. The architect reviews the request and decides the next step.

Can I approve every post and case study?

Yes. BabyRock uses practice-provided photos and notes, and the practice approves public details, permissions and credits.

How does owner takeover work for an important project conversation?

From the owner WhatsApp, choose Take over on the identified conversation. BabyRock pauses that Instagram or Facebook customer thread, lets you reply personally, and relays your reply through the original channel with a receipt. Automation resumes only when you ask.

Does a post on Instagram and Facebook count twice?

No. Cross-posting the same content to Instagram and Facebook counts as one content item.

How does the trial of the Grow plan work?

At launch, the trial is opt-in, lasts 14 days, needs no card and begins once the account is connected and usable. If you do not continue, the account returns to the free plan.

What happens after I join for free?

You reserve launch access. We will let you know when your account can be connected, then you can start with a completed project.
