Your voice, your brand DMs and your next collaboration in one workflow

BabyRock keeps Instagram and Facebook comments personal, brings the brand DMs that need you into one clear queue and turns collaboration messages into details you can act on. Why BabyRock

When brand opportunities are buried under comments and DMs, BabyRock gives you one workflow for replies, messages, collaboration details and image posts. You keep your voice and approve every commercial term.

Keep every public reply recognisably yours

Comments under a campaign, launch or photo are where your audience meets you. BabyRock prepares public replies from your voice examples, favourite phrases and boundaries, so routine questions sound like you. You review the voice and take over any reply that needs your personal judgement.

See important brand DMs before they go cold

Brand opportunities often begin as short DMs. BabyRock brings important Instagram and Facebook DMs into your owner workflow instead of losing them among fan messages. Automated private replies go only to people who contacted you first and cover approved FAQs and links, while anything personal or commercial comes to you.

What the free plan and the Grow plan include

The free plan includes 500 public reply threads a month, 50 automated private conversations for approved FAQs and links, important DMs surfaced, 3 approved Instagram or Facebook image posts a month, and a weekly factual insight.

The Grow plan costs €39.99/month including VAT and includes 5,000 public reply threads, 300 automated private conversations with structured qualification, 12 approved image posts a month, request capture with owner reminders and response tracking, a weekly plan and next-post draft, and owner WhatsApp takeover.

Launch terms are planned for the offer described here. Access opens at launch.

Turn a collaboration message into a brief you can act on

A useful collaboration record starts with brand, deliverables, deadline, compensation and contact. The Grow plan turns an inbound brand message into a structured request, reminds you and tracks the response. It can prepare a personal reply, while you approve every negotiation and commercial term. In WhatsApp takeover, you pause the identified Instagram or Facebook thread, send your own reply and see the delivery receipt; the workflow resumes only when you ask.

A five step workflow from comment to collaboration

1. Connect your Instagram and Facebook accounts. 2. Set the replies, links and boundaries that sound like you. 3. Let routine comments and first-contact DMs receive a prepared answer or come to you. 4. Review collaboration terms and approve your reply. 5. Use the weekly plan to choose the next image post and keep follow-up visible.

Collaboration moments, clearly illustrated

Example one: a skincare brand asks about three feed image posts, a June deadline, a fee range, paid usage and a contract contact. The Grow plan captures those details, flags the fee and usage terms and keeps the reply visible.

Example two: a follower asks where a jacket is from. A public reply in your tone points to the answer, while an approved link can be sent privately to someone who messaged first.

Example three: a brand asks to reuse your campaign photo in paid social. You check the usage rights, approve or decline and keep control of the image. The examples show the collaboration path in practice.

Keep posting visible without building a content factory

Publishing starts with assets you already own. The free plan establishes a consistent image-post rhythm and a weekly factual insight; the Grow plan adds a fuller weekly plan and a next-post draft or brief. Cross-posting the same image to Instagram and Facebook counts as one content item. You provide the images and approve the wording.

Choose the free plan or the Grow plan for the work you want handled

The free plan suits a creator who wants personal replies, a visible inbox and a simple posting rhythm. The Grow plan suits a creator handling more comments, brand conversations and a steadier publishing schedule. The Grow plan also adds structured request tracking and weekly planning. The opt-in trial of the Grow plan lets you test the fuller workflow once your account is connected and usable.

Questions owners ask

What does social media management for influencers include?

It covers public Instagram and Facebook comment replies, important inbound DMs, approved image posts, a weekly view of what needs attention and, on the Grow plan, structured collaboration follow-through. You decide personal replies and every commercial term.

Will Instagram comments still sound like me?

Yes. Give BabyRock voice examples, preferred phrases and boundaries. Routine replies follow that guidance, and you can take over any comment that needs your personal touch.

How does BabyRock handle brand collaboration DMs?

It brings important brand messages into your owner workflow. On the Grow plan, it captures brand, deliverables, deadline, compensation and contact as a structured request, reminds you and tracks the response. You approve every term before anything is agreed.

How do I approve a reply before it goes out?

BabyRock prepares the reply and presents it for approval in the owner workflow. You can edit the wording, approve it or handle the conversation yourself. Published posts and commercial replies follow the approvals you set.

Can I answer an important DM myself through WhatsApp?

The Grow plan's WhatsApp takeover pauses the identified Instagram or Facebook customer thread, relays your reply through that original channel and records the delivery receipt. The workflow resumes only when you ask it to.

Can BabyRock create posts from photos I already own?

Yes, for approved Instagram and Facebook image posts. You provide and approve the images and wording. Cross-posting the same image to both channels counts as one content item.

How does BabyRock keep brand enquiries from getting lost?

Important DMs are surfaced in your owner workflow instead of disappearing among fan messages. The Grow plan keeps the brand, deliverables, deadline, compensation and contact together, with reminders and response tracking so follow-through stays visible.

What does the weekly coach do for an influencer?

The free plan gives you a weekly factual insight and one suggestion. The Grow plan adds a weekly plan, a next-post draft or brief, reminders and a review of results. The coaching supports your judgement without making decisions for you.

How do I join and what happens with the trial of the Grow plan?

Join for free to reserve launch access. We will let you know when your account can be connected. The trial of the Grow plan is opt in, needs no card and starts once your account is connected and usable; if you do not continue, you return to the free plan.
