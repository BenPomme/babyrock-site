Why BabyRock

BabyRock gives your practice a calmer public front door, with one workflow for routine questions, reputation and appointment requests. Start on the free plan for everyday coverage, then use the Grow plan for structured intake, reminders and response tracking when your team needs more support.

Patients look for clarity before they get in touch

Opening hours, location, parking, accessibility, services and what to bring are ordinary questions, but an unclear answer can turn a simple step into a delay. BabyRock keeps your approved practice details and links consistent across Google, Instagram and Facebook, and answers routine nonclinical questions in your practice's voice. Anything that calls for clinical judgment goes directly to a qualified member of your team.

Protect your reputation with replies that sound like you

Reviews and comments shape what a prospective patient sees before they call, so a prompt and measured reply matters. BabyRock prepares routine public replies in your practice voice and brings important DMs to the owner in WhatsApp. Sensitive reviews, complaints and clinical questions arrive with the thread context needed for a person to respond well.

What the free plan and the Grow plan include

The free plan includes 500 public reply threads a month, 50 automated private conversations for approved FAQs and links, important DMs surfaced, 3 approved Instagram or Facebook image posts a month, and a weekly factual insight.

The Grow plan costs €39.99/month including VAT and includes 5,000 public reply threads, 300 automated private conversations with structured qualification, 12 approved image posts a month, request capture with owner reminders and response tracking, a weekly plan and next-post draft, and owner WhatsApp takeover.

Turn appointment requests into an owner-confirmed next step

With the Grow plan, a request can arrive with the administrative details your team needs: name, contact details, service, preferred location and timing. BabyRock keeps the request visible, reminds the owner and tracks the response so a busy morning does not bury it. Your practice checks the request and confirms the appointment, keeping the final decision with the people who know the diary.

A five-step workflow built around your team

First, approve the public facts and a focused library of nonclinical FAQs. Second, connect the supported Google, Instagram and Facebook channels you use. Third, choose which routine private questions can receive an automated answer. Fourth, route appointment requests, sensitive reviews and clinical questions to the practice owner or the right colleague. Fifth, review recurring questions each week and turn approved professional material into the next educational post.

Educational posts from material you already trust

BabyRock prepares image posts from nonclinical material your practice owns and has approved, such as service explanations, access notes or preparation guidance. The practice keeps editorial control: you choose the images and wording, review each draft and approve it for Instagram or Facebook. BabyRock turns the same approved content into a steady posting rhythm, and cross-posting one image to both channels counts as one content item. The workflow stays on nonclinical material, so patient stories and private case details remain out of public content.

Three illustrative administrative moments

Review example: a public review mentions a visit and a long wait. BabyRock prepares a reply that thanks the person, acknowledges the experience and invites a private conversation, without confirming care details or discussing health. The owner decides whether to send it.

DM example: someone asks whether the practice offers evening appointments and step-free access. BabyRock answers from the approved nonclinical FAQ and shares the approved directions link, while keeping the reason for the visit out of the conversation.

Request example: a prospective patient asks for an appointment at the north branch on Tuesday after 17:00 and leaves a phone number. The Grow plan collects the service and timing preference, keeps the request visible for the owner and tracks the response until the practice confirms the next step. These examples show the workflow, not results.

Questions owners ask

How does BabyRock handle medical questions?

Questions about symptoms, diagnosis, treatment or urgency go to a qualified professional on your team. BabyRock manages approved nonclinical administration such as opening hours, access, services, preparation information and appointment requests, so your clinical team can focus on care.

Can BabyRock help with appointment requests?

Yes. With the Grow plan, it can collect the administrative details your practice needs, maintain the request as a case, remind the owner and track the response. Your practice confirms the appointment, so the next step always comes from your team.

What can automated private conversations cover?

Routine nonclinical questions such as hours, location, parking, accessibility, services, approved links and how to request an appointment. The conversation stays on administrative details that your practice has approved.

How does the owner WhatsApp takeover work?

When an owner takes over a conversation from WhatsApp, BabyRock pauses the identified customer thread, relays the owner's reply through the original Instagram or Facebook channel with a receipt and resumes automation only when the owner asks. The customer stays in the original conversation while the owner acts from a familiar place.

How does BabyRock protect patient privacy?

Private conversations collect only the administrative details needed for the request. Public replies keep care details out of view, and sensitive or clinical threads move to a person with the context needed to respond. Your practice's approved material stays the source for public content, with no patient stories or private case details.

Can BabyRock reply to Google reviews?

Yes, when your practice has an eligible connected Google Business Profile. Routine replies use your practice voice, while sensitive reviews and complaints come to the owner with the thread context.

Can we publish educational posts from our own material?

Yes. Your practice supplies and approves nonclinical images and wording, and BabyRock prepares image posts for Instagram and Facebook from that material. Each draft comes back for approval, and cross-posting the same image to both channels counts as one content item.

What does the Grow plan add for a health practice?

The Grow plan expands the monthly public and private capacity, adds structured appointment requests with owner reminders and response tracking, and includes a weekly plan with a next-post draft and the owner WhatsApp takeover flow. The comparison table shows the launch allowances.
