Why BabyRock

BabyRock connects the photo, the post, the guest question and the reservation request in one owner-led rhythm. The free plan keeps the everyday essentials moving, while the Grow plan adds fuller request handling, a more active posting plan and owner takeover from WhatsApp.

Turn the dishes, room and team you already have into a social presence

Send a recent plate, room or team photo with the dish name, price and one useful detail. BabyRock turns your material into on-brand Instagram and Facebook image posts, ready for your approval before anything goes live.

You keep control of the menu, prices, hours and tone, so the post sounds like your restaurant rather than a stock template.

Answer the questions that stand between a guest and a visit

Menu changes, opening hours, terrace seating, party size and accessibility needs arrive across comments, private messages and Google reviews. BabyRock answers routine questions from facts and links you approve, then brings forward the conversations that need a person.

Allergy and health questions go directly to the owner. The first answer stays quick without turning a sensitive guest concern into a guess.

The Grow plan costs €39.99/month including VAT and includes 5,000 public reply threads, 300 automated private conversations with structured qualification, 12 approved image posts a month, request capture with owner reminders and response tracking, a weekly plan and next-post draft, and owner WhatsApp takeover.

Make every reservation request easy to decide

Good reservation details include date, preferred time, party size, seating preference, occasion and any accessibility need. BabyRock gathers them into one clear request for the owner instead of leaving the conversation scattered across channels.

The owner confirms, offers another time or takes over. The guest hears a confirmation only after that decision, using the details you have approved.

A five step restaurant workflow from photo to confirmed request

1. Send the material. Share recent photos, dish names, prices, hours and approved links.

2. Approve the plan. Review the posts, captions and service details before scheduling.

3. Publish and answer. Approved image posts go through supported Instagram and Facebook connections; routine questions receive on-brand replies.

4. Capture the request. Collect date, time, party size, seating and notes, then send one compact request to the owner.

5. Confirm and follow through. The owner confirms or takes over. The Grow plan holds the request, sends reminders and tracks the answer. When the owner takes over from WhatsApp, BabyRock pauses the identified guest thread, sends the reply through the original Instagram or Facebook channel with a receipt, and resumes only when the owner asks.

Three restaurant moments this workflow is built for

A menu change. The owner sends a few photos, the dish names, prices and the set-menu time. BabyRock prepares the post for approval. When a guest asks whether the soup is vegetarian, the public reply uses the approved ingredient note. A serious allergy question goes to the owner.

A Friday reservation. A guest writes with a party size, preferred time, a request for the terrace and a birthday note. BabyRock captures the details for the owner, who confirms, offers an indoor time or asks a focused question. The guest hears the confirmation only after that decision.

A review about a long wait. BabyRock drafts a calm, specific reply without inventing an excuse. The owner edits or takes over, and the weekly insight can flag repeated comments about wait times.

Questions owners ask

What does BabyRock handle for a restaurant?

It combines Instagram and Facebook image posts made from your photos, public comment and Google review replies for eligible profiles, routine private answers, important message surfacing and reservation requests. The Grow plan adds request tracking, a weekly plan and owner takeover from WhatsApp.

Can BabyRock use photos of our dishes, room and team?

Yes. Send owner-provided photos with dish names, prices and the details you want guests to know. BabyRock prepares the post and caption for your approval before publication.

Will replies sound like our restaurant?

BabyRock writes from your approved facts, links and tone. You can review the voice and edit any post or reply before it goes out, so routine answers stay consistent and personal without sounding generic.

How are reservation requests handled?

BabyRock collects the date, preferred time, party size, seating preference and notes, then sends one compact request to the owner. The owner confirms, proposes another time or takes over. No table is confirmed until the owner approves, and BabyRock does not hold table inventory.

Can BabyRock answer menu and allergy questions?

Routine menu questions use the facts and links you approve. Allergy and health questions go to the owner for a direct answer, so a sensitive guest concern is never handled by guesswork.

Does BabyRock manage Google reviews as well as Instagram and Facebook?

Yes, for eligible restaurants with a Google Business Profile. Google review replies sit alongside supported Instagram and Facebook comment replies, with sensitive cases sent to the owner.

How does owner takeover from WhatsApp work?

The owner starts from WhatsApp. BabyRock pauses the identified guest thread on the original Instagram or Facebook channel, sends the owner reply back through that channel with a receipt, and resumes automation only when the owner asks.

What is the difference between the free plan and the Grow plan?

The free plan includes 500 public replies, 50 automated private conversations, 3 approved image posts, important message surfacing and weekly factual insight with one suggestion. The Grow plan is 39.99 EUR a month including VAT with 5,000 public replies, 300 automated private conversations and 12 approved image posts, plus a weekly plan, next-post draft or brief, reminders, response tracking and owner WhatsApp takeover.
