Why BabyRock

For salons, barbers and independent beauty professionals, BabyRock connects the portfolio photo, practical question, appointment request and owner decision in one calm workflow.

Put bookable salon work at the centre of your feed

A fresh cut, colour, barbering or beauty result says more than stock photography. Send a recent photo with the service and approved detail. BabyRock turns owner-provided assets into Instagram and Facebook image posts for approval.

Use images the salon has permission and rights to publish, and check every image and caption before it goes live.

Answer practical questions before a client reaches the chair

Clients ask what a service includes, how pricing works, whether there is space and what to bring to a consultation. BabyRock answers routine questions from owner-approved facts and links.

Condition-dependent prices point to the approved starting price and a consultation. Availability uses the windows and rules you supply; unclear diary questions go to you, not to a guessed calendar.

Three salon moments this workflow is built for

A colour consultation. A returning client asks about softer colour before a wedding, mentions dark box dye and sends inspiration photos. BabyRock captures current colour, timing, budget and preferred windows. You review colour history and confirm a consultation from the real diary. No formula or result is promised in the chat.

A wedding hair request. A bride asks for four people at a venue, a morning ceremony and a trial. BabyRock collects date, location, party size, inspiration and timing needs. You check team availability, travel and the quote, then confirm or propose another plan.

A price and service question. Someone asks whether a gloss includes a blow-dry, what a curly cut costs and whether Saturday is open. Routine facts come from approved notes; a complex quote or unclear slot becomes a short request for you.

Keep reviews personal and important messages visible

Review replies should sound like the person who remembers the visit. Eligible salons can add Google review replies to supported Instagram and Facebook responses, using approved facts and a calm, specific tone.

Important DMs and sensitive comments are surfaced instead of buried. Health, skin, allergy and treatment questions stay with the qualified owner. BabyRock does not publish patient details, provide clinical advice or promise clinical outcomes.

Questions owners ask

What does BabyRock do for a hair or beauty business?

It prepares approved Instagram and Facebook image posts from your portfolio, answers routine service and price questions, surfaces important messages, supports eligible Google review replies and brings appointment or consultation requests to the owner.

Can BabyRock use photos of our salon work?

Yes, with owner-provided photos and permission to publish them. Send the image, service and approved details. BabyRock prepares the post and caption, and you approve both. A cross-post to Instagram and Facebook counts once.

How do appointment and consultation requests reach the owner?

BabyRock collects the service, preferred times, stylist, contact details and relevant notes. The owner checks the real diary and confirms, offers another time or takes over. No appointment is confirmed before that decision.

Can BabyRock answer price, service and availability questions?

Routine questions use owner-approved price and service facts and links. A quote that depends on length, colour history or treatment time goes to the owner. Availability uses the windows the salon supplies, and the owner checks the diary before confirming.

Will replies sound like our salon?

Copy follows the tone, phrases and boundaries you provide. You can review replies and step in when a client needs a more personal answer. The aim is a useful, recognisable voice, not a generic template.

How does owner takeover from WhatsApp work?

The owner takes control from WhatsApp. BabyRock pauses the identified original Instagram or Facebook customer thread, sends the reply through that same channel with a receipt, and resumes only when the owner requests it.

How are reviews and sensitive messages handled?

Eligible salons can add Google review replies to supported Instagram and Facebook responses with owner approval. Important DMs are surfaced. Health, allergy, skin or treatment questions stay with the qualified owner, and patient details are not published publicly.

What happens at launch, and how does the trial of the Grow plan work?

Join for free to reserve launch access. BabyRock will let you know when your account can be connected. At launch, the trial of the Grow plan is opt-in, takes no card and starts once connected and usable. If you do not continue, the account returns to the free plan.
