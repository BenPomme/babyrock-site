A creator workflow that protects your voice and your time

BabyRock turns the repetitive side of a creator business into one clear Instagram and Facebook workflow: routine community replies in your voice, important DMs surfaced, approved links to your offers and a steady rhythm of image posts. UGC and freelance briefs arrive with the details you need to decide, while you keep control of your voice, assets and commercial terms. Why BabyRock

When community questions, offers and client work compete for the same hours, BabyRock brings replies, links, requests and posts into one owner-controlled workflow.

Keep your community conversation moving

Your community asks about tutorials, course access, membership details and where to find your work. BabyRock prepares routine replies from your voice examples, handles approved FAQ and link messages from people who contacted you first, and surfaces the conversations that need you. You can step in at any point without dropping the thread.

Turn interest into the right next step

Your courses, memberships, templates and products are offers you already own. Add approved answers, approved links and eligibility notes so a public reply can point people to the right resource and an automated private reply can help someone who messaged first. You decide which links are available, how they are described and when they change.

What the free plan and the Grow plan include

The free plan includes 500 public reply threads a month, 50 automated private conversations for approved FAQs and links, important DMs surfaced, 3 approved Instagram or Facebook image posts a month, and a weekly factual insight.

The Grow plan costs €39.99/month including VAT and includes 5,000 public reply threads, 300 automated private conversations with structured qualification, 12 approved image posts a month, request capture with owner reminders and response tracking, a weekly plan and next-post draft, and owner WhatsApp takeover.

Launch terms are planned for the offer described here. Access opens at launch.

Capture UGC and freelance briefs before they stall

A UGC or freelance request needs a clear record of the client, deliverables, deadline, fee or compensation, usage rights and contact. The Grow plan collects those details in a structured request, reminds you to respond and tracks what happens next. You approve the scope, commercial terms, credit and final reply.

One workflow from message to approved post

1. Connect your supported Instagram and Facebook accounts. 2. Add voice examples, recurring answers and approved links. 3. Let routine comments and first-contact DMs receive a prepared answer or come to you for a decision. 4. Capture client briefs and approve the terms. 5. Review the weekly coach and approve each image post before it goes out.

Three creator enquiries, clearly illustrated

Example one: someone asks whether your course is open to beginners outside your country. A public reply uses your wording, and the approved eligibility page goes privately to the person who messaged first.

Example two: a clothing brand asks for two sets of product photos, paid ad usage, a launch deadline, a fee range and a contract contact. The Grow plan captures the brief and sends the terms to you for approval.

Example three: you publish a new template pack using a photo you own, an approved description and a checkout link. The workflow prepares the post, and final approval stays with you. Each example keeps the decision in your hands.

Build a posting rhythm you can sustain

Publishing starts with images you already own or are cleared to use. The free plan supports a light image-post rhythm and a weekly factual insight; the Grow plan adds a fuller posting schedule, a weekly plan, a next-post draft or brief, reminders and a review of results. Cross-posting the same image to Instagram and Facebook counts as one content item. You provide the image and approve the wording before publication.

Stay in control when a conversation needs you

On the Grow plan, owner WhatsApp takeover lets you reply from WhatsApp while BabyRock pauses the identified customer thread on Instagram or Facebook, relays your reply through the original channel and shows a delivery receipt. The workflow resumes only when you ask. Voice examples, approved links, image assets and client terms stay under your control, so the account keeps your judgement as well as your tone.

Choose the free plan or the Grow plan for your creator workload

The free plan is built for a creator who wants routine community replies, approved offer links, important DMs surfaced and a light posting rhythm. The Grow plan is for a busier community, active UGC or freelance work and a more consistent publishing plan, with structured request capture, reminders, response tracking, a weekly plan and owner WhatsApp takeover. Both plans keep your voice and approval in the workflow; the Grow plan adds the follow-through for more conversations and client work.

Questions owners ask

What does BabyRock handle for a content creator?

It brings Instagram and Facebook community replies, important inbound DMs, approved links to your own offers, UGC or freelance request details and owner-approved image posts into one workflow.

Will replies and posts still sound like me?

Yes. You provide voice examples, preferred phrases and boundaries. BabyRock prepares routine copy from that guidance, and you can take over personal conversations or approve each post before publication.

Can I share links to my courses, memberships or products?

Yes. Add the links you own to your approved library, with a short note on where each belongs. Automated private replies go only to people who contacted you first, and you decide when a link changes or comes out of the library.

How does BabyRock handle UGC or freelance briefs?

The Grow plan captures the client, deliverables, deadline, fee or compensation, usage rights and contact in a structured request. It reminds you to respond and tracks the follow-through, while you approve the scope, terms and final reply.

How does owner WhatsApp takeover work?

On the Grow plan, you reply from WhatsApp while BabyRock pauses the identified Instagram or Facebook customer thread, relays your reply through the original channel with a delivery receipt and resumes only when you ask.

Can I approve every image post and use my own assets?

Yes. Image posts use photos and material you provide or are cleared to use, and you approve the wording and image before publication. Cross-posting the same content to Instagram and Facebook counts as one content item.

What is the difference between the free plan and the Grow plan?

The free plan covers routine community replies, approved offer links, important DMs, a light image-post rhythm and a weekly factual insight. The Grow plan expands the conversation and posting capacity and adds structured request capture, reminders, response tracking, a weekly plan with a next-post draft or brief, and owner WhatsApp takeover.

How does the 14-day trial of the Grow plan work?

The planned trial of the Grow plan is opt-in, lasts 14 days, needs no card once your account is connected and usable, and returns you to the free plan if you do not continue.

How do I join, and when can I connect my accounts?

Join for free to reserve launch access. We will let you know when your account can be connected; signup remains on the waitlist until access opens.

Who approves UGC fees and client terms?

You do. BabyRock keeps the request and follow-through together, while you decide availability, scope, fee, usage rights and the final reply. Your approval confirms every client booking and commercial term.
