The free plan and your first 1,000 followers

Start free. Grow at your pace.

Get started with comments, reviews and DM alerts. Move to a paid plan when you want more.

Free social services cover accounts with up to 1,000 Instagram followers.

If your connected account passes 1,000 followers, we’ll notify you and give you 30 days to choose a paid plan. After that, Instagram and Facebook comment replies and DM alerts pause if you stay on Free.

Google review replies remain free. Your dashboard and history stay available. Choose a paid plan to resume your social services.

Social services require a connected Instagram account. You can use Google review replies without connecting Instagram.
