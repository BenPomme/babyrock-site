What the free plan does

  • Google review replies and Instagram and Facebook comment replies in your brand voice
  • DM alerts for up to 100 conversations a month, without automatic DM replies
  • Google reviews rated 1 to 3 stars come to you for approval; if you do not answer within 48 hours, the reply is published
  • Your dashboard and weekly activity recap
  • Free social services up to 1,000 Instagram followers, with a 30 day grace period after a verified crossing

What you need to start:

  • A working WhatsApp number, so Rosalía can reach you
  • An email address for the account and the invoices
  • Once selected, you will need to add us as manager of the social platforms you need us to manage (Google, Instagram and Facebook). We will guide you through it.

No payment card is required to start. No app to install. You keep the ownership of every account, and you can remove us at any time.
