2. The service

BabyRock Social looks after the online presence of one shop from the WhatsApp you already have, in four cumulative plans:

  • Lite: we answer every review on your Google and Maps listing, keep its hours, holidays, details and photos current, post four times a month on Instagram and Facebook from what you send us, and watch your website with four small edits a month.
  • Plus: everything in Lite, with a person on our team checking every post and every reply before it is published, twelve posts a month on Instagram and Facebook, private messages up to 500 a month, and small edits to your site with no limit.
  • Pro: everything in Plus, with posts and Google posts with no monthly limit, and your website rebuilt, maintained and edited, with its pages and titles prepared for Google.
  • Enterprise: everything in Pro, with up to three locations on one account and one invoice, priority in the queue and custom message volume. Each extra location, 100 € a month.

Reviews follow these rules, on every plan:

  • We import unanswered reviews (up to the 20 most recent at start, unless a different offer applies; then the live stream).
  • The draft is written in the language of the review (Spanish, Catalan, French or English).
  • On Plus, Pro and Enterprise, a person we authorise reads every reply and, if needed, edits it before it is published. AI may help with the first draft. Nobody publishes text a person has not seen. On Lite, you approve the 1, 2 and 3 star replies yourself.
  • 4- and 5-star reviews: published under the standing authorisation you give by subscribing and inviting us as manager.
  • 1-, 2- and 3-star reviews: published only after you approve or send the text on BabyRock WhatsApp (Fil Babyrock). Silence is not yes.
  • Included: a WhatsApp thread with you (1 to 3 star drafts, recap, billing, listing alerts), the Monday recap, a same-day ping if hours, name, phone, address or status change, and a ping if a review in our journal disappears from the listing (we do not republish it).
  • Public holidays: we tell you. You say CERRADO (we patch via API) or you change it in Google. The operator does not change hours on their own.

You invite reviews@babyrock.ai as manager, not as owner. We never ask for the Google password.

3. What is not included

Paid advertising, photo or video shoots at your premises, influencer work, booking management, online shops, third party platforms such as TheFork or TripAdvisor, sales calls, copy-paste to an assistant, the shop’s WhatsApp with its customers (BabyRock Direct, not sold today), asking for reviews after a visit, phone lists, a promise of more reviews or a better rating, competitor spying, directories other than Google, listing attributes, or Google Local Posts.

The site guides teach habits you can do yourself. They are not part of this contract.

5. How you contract and pay

The contract is made online at app.babyrock.ai/pay. You tick that you accept these terms and the privacy policy, and you pay by card (or SEPA / Bizum if enabled) through Stripe. Stripe charges periodically until you cancel.

A payment link by WhatsApp or email is an invitation to contract. The contract is formed when you accept and complete payment or the trial sign-up.

6. Term, renewal and cancellation

The subscription is month to month or year to year, depending on the plan, and renews by itself.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid. Ask Rosalia on WhatsApp or write to contact@babyrock.ai. Replies already published stay on Google. When the service ends, we remove our manager access. You can also remove us at any time in Google (Business Profile settings → People and access → reviews@babyrock.ai → Remove person).

If a trial does not convert to payment, we stop reply work.

If a charge fails, we may pause the service until it is regularised.

Beta phase (BETA). BabyRock Social is in beta: we may pause or end the service with 30 days written notice by email and WhatsApp. From the day of the notice we stop selling new subscriptions. A monthly subscription runs to the end of the month already paid and is not refunded. An annual subscription is refunded pro rata for the months not served. Data is exported on request.

No refund of the current period, except the pro rata refund of the annual subscription set out in the beta paragraph and unless a mandatory rule requires it.

By subscribing and inviting us as manager, you authorise us to draft, review, submit, edit and, if needed, delete replies on that listing, under article 2.

Google reviews each reply and decides when it is public. A Google refusal or delay is not our breach.

You remain responsible for the listing. If it is unverified, if you remove our access, or if Google limits the API, the service suffers or stops until that is fixed.

8. Artificial intelligence

We use AI tools to help prepare drafts. A person reviews and, if needed, edits every reply before it is published. The published text is the shop’s reply, not content generated and sent blind.
