Benjamin Pommeraud, founder

Shop owners, restaurant owners, salon owners: they're brilliant at what they do, but nobody has the time to be great at their craft and run their own marketing. Big brands have teams for that. BabyRock gives every owner an assistant that does it for them.

Rosalia, your marketing assistant

She lives in your WhatsApp and handles the marketing you never have time for. Today she runs your Google profile: she answers every review in your customer's language, writes your weekly posts and prepares your photos. Next: Instagram, Facebook, customer messages, orders and bookings. She's an AI assistant, and she's available for you 24/7.

You stay in control

Rosalia replies to good reviews on her own, so you don't have to. Anything sensitive, like a 1 to 3 star review or a new post, waits for your OK on WhatsApp. Change or switch off anything, anytime.
