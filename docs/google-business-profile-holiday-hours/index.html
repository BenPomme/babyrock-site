Google Business Profile holiday hours: Christmas and New Year 2026

How to set special hours on Google, with the UK bank holiday dates for Christmas and New Year.

Updated 25 November 2026 · By the BabyRock team

The short answer

To set holiday hours, go to business.google.com, select Edit profile, then Hours, and use the pencil icon beside Special hours. Pick each date, enter your opening times or leave Closed ticked, and select Save. Google also recommends confirming hours for official holidays even when they match your usual ones, so customers can trust what they see.

The steps

  1. Open your profileGo to business.google.com and sign in to your Business Profile.
  2. Edit profile, then HoursSelect Edit profile, then Hours.
  3. Find Special hoursIn the Business hours section, select the pencil icon next to Special hours.
  4. Pick the dateConfirm a suggested date with Review, choose Add a date for your own, or select an existing date to change it.
  5. Set the hoursIf you're open, untick Closed and enter your times. If you're shut, leave Closed ticked. To add a second set of hours for the same day, select More, then Add hours.
  6. SaveSelect Save. Your special hours show under Special hours in Edit profile.

These dates come from the official list of UK bank holidays on gov.uk, checked on 25 November 2026. Boxing Day and 2 January land on a Saturday this year, so watch the Monday substitute days.

For every row you plan to treat differently, add a special hours entry: closed, or the reduced times you'll actually keep. Do the same for days that match your normal hours, because Google recommends confirming hours for official holidays either way.

Limits that catch people out

Google's page on special hours sets out the rules. These are the ones that cause the most mistakes:

  • Special hours are for up to six days in a row. If you'll be closed for seven days or more, or for an unknown time, mark the business as Temporarily closed instead.
  • One set of hours can't run past 24 hours. If you open after midnight, split it into two sets: one ending at midnight and one starting at midnight the next day. The next day's regular hours are then ignored, so re-enter any you want to keep.
  • Your hours must be set to Open with main hours for special hours to show on your profile.
  • Different times for different services, such as delivery or senior hours, belong under More hours, not under special hours.

What happens if you don't set them

Google doesn't describe a penalty. The practical risk is simpler: if you don't add special hours, your profile shows your regular hours for that day. A customer who sees you open on Christmas Day and finds a locked door will not blame Google, and may leave a one-star review.

Google also reviews edits before they go live, so don't leave it to the last day. Aim to have everything saved by the start of December and look at your profile on a phone afterwards to check it reads correctly.

A short festive checklist

  • Choose your hours for each date in the table and write them down once, so the shop window, website and Google all say the same.
  • Add the special hours on Google, including days that match your usual hours.
  • Check the profile on your phone in Search and in Maps.
  • Make sure the phone number on the profile is one someone will answer. Google may use your verified phone number to confirm information.
  • In the new year, check that normal hours are showing again and look at any reviews that mention opening times.

Frequently asked questions

Is Christmas Eve a UK bank holiday?

No. The official gov.uk list has Christmas Day and the Boxing Day substitute day, not Christmas Eve. Set your own hours for it.

When is the Boxing Day bank holiday in 2026?

Boxing Day falls on Saturday 26 December 2026, so the bank holiday is Monday 28 December, according to gov.uk.

Is 2 January a bank holiday?

Only in Scotland. In 2027 it falls on a Saturday, so the substitute day is Monday 4 January 2027.

How many days can special hours cover?

Google says special hours are for temporary changes of up to six days in a row. For seven days or more, or an unknown period, mark the business as temporarily closed.

Do I need to add special hours if I'm open as normal?

Google recommends confirming hours for official holidays even if they match your regular hours, so customers know your hours are accurate.

Why aren't my special hours showing?

Check that your hours are set to Open with main hours, and remember that Google reviews changes before they update live on your profile.

Can I set special hours for several holidays at once?

Yes. Add a date for each one, or confirm the dates Google suggests with Review, and save.

Does this work in Scotland and Northern Ireland?

Yes. The steps are the same everywhere. Only the bank holiday dates differ, and the table above shows which ones apply where.
