How to use these templates without sounding like a template

A template is a starting point. Customers read dozens of replies, and a block of identical wording across your profile is easy to spot. Google also expects replies to be genuine: its help page on replying to reviews asks businesses to keep replies relevant and polite.

Before you paste anything, change three things:

  • One concrete detail from the review. The dish, the job, the person, the day. If you can't find one, the review is probably a short one and a short reply is right.
  • Your own phrase. If you never say "valued customer", don't start now. Read it aloud. If it doesn't sound like you at the till, rewrite it.
  • Something that happened. "I've told the kitchen" or "I'm checking the rota" is better than "we take feedback seriously", and it must be true.

Rotate openings as well. If your last five replies begin "Thank you for your review", the sixth will look automated. Start some with the person's name, some with the detail, some with the apology.

The five-part structure behind every template

Each reply below follows the same skeleton, which is also in our guide to responding to Google reviews.

  1. 1. Thank them, by nameUse their first name if the review shows one.
  2. 2. Name one specific thingA detail only someone who was there would know.
  3. 3. Own what went wrongOne plain sentence, with no "but" in it.
  4. 4. Say what happens nextA concrete step or a private way to contact you.
  5. 5. Sign off brieflyFirst name and role. No opening hours, no discount.

Positive reviews only need parts 1, 2 and 5. Complaints need all five. For a more detailed method on difficult reviews, see how to respond to negative Google reviews.

Five-star reviews with a comment

Short, warm and specific. Repeat one detail from the review so it is obvious a person read it.

Example · 1. Praise for the food or product

Review

Loved the [dish]. Perfect portion and arrived hot.

Reply

Thanks, [first name]. I'll tell the kitchen the [dish] hit the spot. It's one of our favourites too. Hope to see you again soon. [your name], owner

Why it works: Echoes the detail, passes the compliment on, no sales pitch.

Example · 2. Praise for the work done

Review

Fitted our new boiler in a day and left the place spotless. Explained everything clearly.

Reply

Thanks, [first name]. Leaving the house as we found it matters as much as the boiler, so I'm glad you noticed. Do get in touch if anything seems off before the first service. [your name]

Why it works: Credits the habit behind the praise and offers a useful next step.

Example · 3. Praise for the atmosphere

Review

Such a relaxed place. We sat for two hours and nobody rushed us.

Reply

That's lovely to hear, [first name]. We'd rather you stayed for a second coffee than felt hurried. Come back whenever you need a quiet table. [your name]

Why it works: Shows the venue's own philosophy, not a stock thank-you.

Example · 4. Praise that names a team member

Review

[staff name] was brilliant with our daughter, who was nervous. Thank you.

Reply

Thank you, [first name]. I've shown [staff name] your review and it made their day. Your daughter is welcome back whenever she's ready. [your name]

Why it works: Uses the staff member's first name only, and proves it was passed on.

Example · 5. Praise with a recommendation

Review

Third time here with visitors. Everyone we bring says the same: go back.

Reply

Thanks, [first name]. Bringing visitors is the best compliment we can get. Please pass on that there's always a seat for friends of regulars. [your name]

Why it works: Treats the recommendation as the real compliment.

Example · 6. Praise from a first-time customer

Review

First visit after finding you on Google Maps. Won't be the last.

Reply

Welcome, [first name], and thanks for taking a chance on a new place. Glad it was worth the search. Next time, ask about whatever's new on the board. [your name]

Why it works: Acknowledges that they found you, then gives a small reason to return.

Example · 7. Praise for a special occasion

Review

Held my mum's 70th here. The staff made it feel special without any fuss.

Reply

Thank you, [first name]. Happy 70th to your mum. Looking after a family on a day like that is what we love doing, and we'd be glad to host the next one. [your name]

Why it works: Names the occasion, which no generic template could.

Five-star reviews with no text, and star-only ratings

Review

Reply

Thank you, [first name]. Pleased you thought so. See you again soon. [your name]

Why it works: Matches the length of the review.

Example · 10. Five stars, only an emoji

Why it works: Brevity is the courtesy here.

Repeat customers and regulars

Regulars deserve a reply that sounds like you know them, without using personal details.

Example · 11. Long-time regular

Review

Been coming for nearly ten years. Still the best part of my Saturday.

Reply

Ten years, [first name]. That means a great deal. You've seen more of our changes than most of the team has. Your usual table is yours whenever you want it. [your name]

Why it works: Acknowledges the length of loyalty in a way that sounds human.

Example · 12. Customer returning after a bad experience

Review

You sorted out the problem from last time and this visit was spot on. Thanks for listening.

Reply

Thanks for giving us another go, [first name]. We took your last comments seriously and it's good to know the changes landed. [your name]

Why it works: Closes the loop on an earlier complaint.

Example · 13. Customer who updates a review upwards

Review

Updated to five stars. They called me the next day and put it right.

Reply

Thank you for updating it, [first name]. We're glad we could put it right, and we appreciate you telling others. [your name]

Why it works: Short and gracious. No victory lap.

Mixed reviews (three and four stars)

Thank them for what worked, deal with the one thing that didn't, and don't argue with the rating.

Example · 14. Good, but slow

Review

Food was great but we waited ages between courses.

Reply

Thanks, [first name]. Glad you liked the food, and sorry about the gaps between courses. That's not the pace we aim for. If you'd like to tell us which evening it was, I'll look at how the team was covered. [your name]

Why it works: Owns the delay and offers a concrete next step.

Example · 15. Good, but pricey

Review

Lovely quality, just felt expensive for what we ordered.

Reply

Thanks, [first name]. Pleased the quality came through. Prices are something we think about carefully, and I appreciate you saying how it felt. Our [set menu / midweek offer] is a lighter way to try us. [your name]

Why it works: Acknowledges without apologising for the price, and points to an option.

Example · 16. Three stars, no comment

Review

(3 stars, no comment)

Reply

Thanks for rating us, [first name]. We'd like to earn a higher score, so if you can tell us what fell short, I'll take it seriously. [your name]

Why it works: Invites detail without sounding hurt.

Example · 17. Four stars, one fix

Review

Everything was lovely, but the music was too loud to talk.

Reply

Thanks, [first name], and good point on the music. I've asked the team to turn it down earlier in the evening. [your name]

Why it works: A small, doable fix stated as a decision.

Example · 18. Praise for the product, complaint about the building

Review

Great treatment but parking was impossible and the waiting area was cold.

Reply

Thank you, [first name]. I'm glad the treatment went well. You're right about the waiting area: we're adding a heater this month. The car park behind the library has spaces after 10am. [your name]

Why it works: Handles two complaints with facts, not excuses.

Slow service and long waits

Apologise once, say what you'll check, and don't blame customers or a busy day.

Example · 19. Slow service on a busy night

Review

Waited 50 minutes for our main courses on Friday. Nobody told us why.

Reply

Sorry, [first name]. Fifty minutes is too long, and nobody telling you was our mistake as well. I'm looking at how we cover Friday evenings. If you'd like to email me your booking details, I'll make sure someone looks into it. [your name]

Why it works: Separates the wait from the lack of communication.

Example · 20. Waited past the appointment time

Review

Arrived on time for my 2pm and wasn't seen until 2.40.

Reply

I'm sorry, [first name]. You should have been told about the delay when you arrived. An earlier appointment ran long, but that doesn't make it acceptable. I've changed how we update the waiting room. [your name]

Why it works: Explains once, briefly, then states what changed.

Example · 21. Nobody answers the phone

Review

Rang four times in a week and never got through.

Reply

Sorry, [first name]. That isn't good enough, and I understand the frustration. We're also on WhatsApp on [number] so you can message at any hour. I'll go through the missed calls. [your name]

Why it works: Offers a different route straight away.

Example · 22. Understaffed

Review

Only two staff for a full room. They did their best but couldn't cope.

Reply

Thank you for being fair to the team, [first name]. You're right that we were short that night, and that's on me, not them. I've changed our rota. [your name]

Why it works: Protects staff and takes responsibility as owner.

Price and value complaints

State the facts calmly. Never call the reviewer cheap.

Example · 23. General price complaint

Review

Overpriced for what it is.

Reply

Thanks, [first name]. We know prices matter and I'm sorry it didn't feel like good value. If you tell me what you ordered, I'll gladly explain how we set our prices. [your name]

Why it works: Invites a conversation rather than defending the menu.

Example · 24. Unexpected extra charge

Review

Got a charge on the bill nobody mentioned.

Reply

Sorry, [first name]. It should have been explained before you paid. Please message me the date and I'll check the bill with you and correct it if it was wrong. [your name]

Why it works: Admits the communication failure and promises an actual check.

Example · 25. Quote higher than expected

Review

Quote was nearly double what I'd guessed. No explanation.

Reply

Thanks, [first name]. I'm sorry the quote came as a surprise. We should have walked you through what's in it. If you'd like, I can send the breakdown line by line. [your name]

Why it works: Offers transparency without caving.

Example · 26. Minimum charge complaint

Review

Charged a minimum even though I only needed ten minutes.

Reply

Thanks for explaining, [first name]. Our minimum call-out covers travel and preparation, and it's on our website, but I should have said it on the phone too. Apologies for the surprise. [your name]

Why it works: Factual and honest about where the fault lay.

Wrong order, damaged items and poor work

Fix first, reply second. A reply that says what you've already done is worth far more than an apology.

Example · 27. Wrong order

Review

Ordered the vegetarian option and was given the one with chicken. Sent it back, still waited ages.

Reply

I'm very sorry, [first name]. Getting the order wrong and then the delay in correcting it is on us. I've reminded the team to repeat dietary choices back at the table. If you email me, I'll put something right. [your name]

Why it works: Names both mistakes and the process change.

Example · 28. Item arrived damaged

Review

Parcel arrived with a cracked lid. No reply from your email.

Reply

Sorry, [first name]. A damaged item and an unanswered email is a bad combination. I've found your message and will send a replacement today. [your name]

Why it works: Shows action already taken.

Example · 29. Work needs redoing

Review

The shelf is crooked and one bracket is loose.

Reply

Thank you for telling us, [first name], and I'm sorry it wasn't right. I'll come back to refit it properly, at no charge. Please message to find a day that suits you. [your name]

Why it works: Commits to a free fix in public, which others will read.

Example · 30. Cold food

Review

My food was cold when it reached the table.

Reply

I'm sorry, [first name]. Cold food shouldn't leave the kitchen, and I've asked the pass to check temperatures again. Please email me, and I'll make it up to you on your next visit. [your name]

Why it works: Short, specific and offers a route back.

Claims about rude staff

Never name or blame staff in public. Say you take it seriously and move it to a private channel.

Example · 31. Rude staff, general

Review

The woman at the till was dismissive and rude.

Reply

I'm sorry, [first name]. That's not how we want anyone treated here. I'd like to understand what happened, so please email me at [address]. [your name], owner

Why it works: Takes it seriously without defending or accusing.

Example · 32. Rude staff, but you have a different account

Review

Manager shouted at us for asking to move tables.

Reply

Thanks for telling us, [first name]. I'm sorry you left with that impression. I wasn't able to find a note of what happened, so I'd value hearing your side by email, and I'll speak to everyone on shift. [your name]

Why it works: Neutral. Doesn't call the reviewer wrong in public.

Example · 33. Complaint about a named employee

Review

[Name] was useless and should be sacked.

Reply

Sorry, [first name], that wasn't a good experience. We don't discuss individual team members in public, but I'll look into it properly. Please contact me directly at [address]. [your name]

Why it works: Declines the public blame, keeps the door open.

Bookings, no-shows and cancellations

Check your own records before you reply. Be factual and polite.

Example · 34. Booking was lost

Review

We had a booking for eight and were told there was none.

Reply

I'm sorry, [first name]. We found the booking had gone in under a different date, and that's our mistake. I'd like to make it right for you. Please email me and we'll sort out a table for your group. [your name]

Why it works: Admits the fault and says what happened.

Example · 35. Customer says you didn't turn up

Review

Booked a call-out for Tuesday. Nobody came, no call.

Reply

Sorry, [first name]. Our diary shows a visit booked for Thursday, so there may have been a mix-up on the day. Either way, I'd like to rebook you at your convenience. Please message me. [your name]

Why it works: Politely states the record without calling them wrong.

Example · 36. Cancellation fee dispute

Review

Charged a fee for cancelling an hour before. Ridiculous.

Reply

Thanks, [first name]. I understand the frustration. The fee follows our cancellation terms, which were sent with the confirmation, but I'm happy to talk through your circumstances. Please contact me at [address]. [your name]

Why it works: Explains the policy once and leaves room to be human.

Difficult cases

Not every review can be fixed with a template. These stay calm, factual and short.

Example · 37. Review in another language

Review

[Review in Polish, five stars]

Reply

Dziękujemy! Thank you for your kind review. We hope to see you again. [your name]

Why it works: A short thank-you in their language and in English. Check any translation before posting.

Example · 38. You can't find this customer

Review

Terrible. They lost my order and wouldn't refund.

Reply

Thank you for your feedback, [first name]. I can't find an order under your name and I'd like to look into it. Please email me with the date and a reference so I can check. [your name]

Why it works: Neutral. It doesn't accuse, and it works if the review is real or mistaken.

Example · 39. Star-only one-star rating

Review

(1 star, no comment)

Reply

We're sorry the experience fell short, [first name]. We'd like to understand what went wrong. If you're willing, please email us at [address]. [your name]

Why it works: Doesn't presume the cause. Offers a private route.

Example · 40. Health or safety concern

Review

I felt unwell after eating here and I think it was the chicken.

Reply

I'm very sorry to hear you were unwell, [first name]. We take this seriously and I'd like to talk to you directly. Please contact me on [phone] so we can look into it properly. [your name]

Why it works: Takes it offline at once. No details in public, no medical comments.

When not to use a template at all

Some reviews need a written answer from you, and sometimes no public answer at all.

  • Anything involving health, injury, safety or the police. Reply briefly, publicly, and move to a phone call or email. Never discuss details or give advice in the reply.
  • Allegations about named people. Don't confirm, deny or repeat the name.
  • Reviews containing personal data, such as a full name, an address or a booking reference. Don't repeat it, and consider reporting the review.
  • Anything that looks fake. Reply once, neutrally, and report it to Google. Our guide to fake Google reviews explains the evidence to collect.
  • Reviews that break Google's rules (spam, hate speech, conflict of interest). You can ask Google to remove these. Replying is optional. See removing Google reviews.
  • An angry customer you can already ring. A phone call usually beats three public messages.

Frequently asked questions

Is it okay to use the same reply for several reviews?

It's better not to. Identical replies are easy to spot and make you look automated. Reuse the structure, but change the detail and the opening each time.

How long should a reply be?

Two to four sentences. Short for star-only and one-word reviews, a little longer for complaints where you need to say what happens next.

Should I use the reviewer's name?

Yes, if the review shows a first name. Use first names only, never a full name, and never include other personal details.

Do I need to apologise for every negative review?

Apologise when something really went wrong. If you can't find the customer or the claim doesn't match your records, stay neutral and ask for details instead.

Can I offer a refund or discount in the reply?

It's usually better to move that to a private message. A public offer can invite other reviewers to ask for the same and doesn't help the original customer.

What if Google rejects my reply?

Google checks replies against its content policies. If a reply isn't approved you can edit it and try again. Avoid links, contact details you wouldn't want public, and anything abusive.
