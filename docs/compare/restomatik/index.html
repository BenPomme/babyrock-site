Restomatik alternative: BabyRock carries the guest relationship beyond the booking

BabyRock gives restaurants a faster, more consistent guest voice around every booking: Instagram and Facebook comment replies, paid inbound DM handling, owner-approved content, website care and review replies. Restomatik provides reservations, guest records and the booking workflow.

Join for free

Why BabyRock

BabyRock is the stronger fit for the public conversation before and after the visit.

Why BabyRock is stronger for the guest conversation after the booking

BabyRock keeps the guest-facing voice moving after the reservation is made. Public Instagram and Facebook comment replies plus DM alerts are included on the free plan. Paid Start at 9.99 EUR adds automated replies for 200 inbound conversations a month, and the Grow plan at 24.99 EUR covers 1,000, with owner escalation when a thread needs judgment.

A guest can ask about a menu change under a photo or send a booking question in a DM and receive a consistent answer in the restaurant's voice. The owner stays close to the conversations that affect the next visit.

Why BabyRock is stronger for a calm inbound DM workflow

Paid inbound DM handling answers routine questions while the owner decides on booking changes, allergies, refunds, large groups or complaints. The owner WhatsApp thread keeps those decisions close to the restaurant and gives the team one place to send corrections, photos and service details.

Routine messages keep moving, sensitive cases return to the owner and the guest gets a useful reply without the restaurant losing control of the details that matter.

Why BabyRock is stronger for content and website updates

Content 12 is an optional 9.99 EUR add-on for 12 owner-approved publications a month, and Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR. Website care at 49.99 EUR a month covers four small edits, security, backups, a monthly report and hosting oversight; a build or rebuild starts at 499 EUR.

The owner can send a menu update, photo or correction through WhatsApp, review the content plan or private preview and approve the final result. Replies, posts and pages then tell one coherent story.

Why BabyRock is stronger for reviews that deserve context

Eligible restaurants can use free Google review replies in the brand voice. Routine four and five star reviews can move automatically, while one to three star reviews start as an owner draft so the person who knows the service can edit or approve the answer.

Restomatik lists post-visit review requests and AI-assisted responses on Pro and Premium, with a website add-on. BabyRock brings the public reviews into an owner-led workflow that also covers social comments, paid inbound DMs, content and website care.

Questions buyers ask

How does BabyRock support a restaurant alongside Restomatik?

BabyRock gives the restaurant a stronger guest-facing voice across social comments, paid inbound DMs, content, website care and review replies. Restomatik runs reservations and the booking workflow.

Are Google review replies free in BabyRock?

Yes. Google review replies are free for eligible businesses with a Google Business Profile. Paid social, content and website services are optional.

How are one to three star reviews handled?

The owner receives a draft first. The default publish window is 48 hours, and a no-deadline option is available when the restaurant prefers to wait.

Does BabyRock answer Instagram and Facebook comments?

Yes. Public comment replies and DM alerts are included on the free plan. Paid Start and the Grow plan add automated replies to inbound DMs.

Are automatic DM replies included on the free plan?

The free plan includes DM alerts. Paid Start and the Grow plan add automatic replies to inbound conversations, and sensitive decisions can be escalated to the owner.

Can BabyRock prepare content and maintain the website?

Yes. Content 12 is 9.99 EUR for 12 publications, Content Unlimited 29.99 EUR and website care 49.99 EUR a month. Builds start at 499 EUR.
