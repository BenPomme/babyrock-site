Wati alternative: BabyRock makes WhatsApp the owner's route to public customer care

BabyRock gives the owner one WhatsApp thread for the decisions that shape the brand: important inbound DMs, review drafts, summaries and listing updates. Public comment replies and DM alerts start free for smaller accounts, paid plans automate replies to inbound people, and optional content and website care keep the customer experience moving. Wati provides a WhatsApp Business API platform for customer messaging, campaigns and shared team inboxes.

Why BabyRock

BabyRock is the stronger choice for an owner who wants WhatsApp to surface the right decisions and keep public replies, reviews, content and the website consistent, with paid capacity added as inbound volume grows.

Why BabyRock is stronger for owner-led WhatsApp coordination

A busy owner needs WhatsApp to keep customer decisions close and moving. BabyRock brings the customer moments that need judgment into one thread: important inbound DMs, review alerts, low-star drafts, summaries and listing updates. Public comment replies and DM alerts start free for smaller accounts, so the owner can see the value in the everyday customer conversation before adding paid capacity.

Routine replies keep moving in the brand voice. Complaints, negotiations and unusual requests arrive with the context the owner needs to make a confident decision. The same workflow connects Instagram and Facebook comments, Google reviews, content and website care, so the owner sees one connected customer story.

Answer in public and give private DMs the right attention

Comments are the public face of customer care. BabyRock replies to Instagram and Facebook comments in the account's voice, handling everyday questions and sending sensitive exchanges to the owner. When a comment becomes a private message, DM alerts make sure the custom order, booking question or collaboration request reaches the person who can act on it.

Start adds automated replies to inbound conversations for 9.99 EUR a month. The Grow plan adds a larger allowance and a second seat for 24.99 EUR. The workflow serves people who contacted the business first, with the owner keeping the decisions that need a personal response.

Plan content, posting and website updates from the same thread

Useful content often starts with a customer question. Content 12 is an optional 9.99 EUR monthly add-on for 12 owner-approved publications. Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR for a higher publication allowance. Website care is 49.99 EUR a month for hosting oversight, security, backups, four small edits and a monthly report. A supported website build or rebuild starts from 499 EUR once.

The owner can send a photo, price change or content direction in plain language, review the result and keep the public voice consistent. BabyRock brings the customer conversation and the work behind it into one relationship, with every post approved before publication.

Give Google reviews the same owner-first standard

Eligible Google review replies are free on BabyRock. Routine four and five star reviews can answer automatically in brand voice. One to three star reviews start with the owner, who can pause a response when it needs more context. Sensitive cases escalate, and no member of the BabyRock team reads every routine reply.

Reviews then sit alongside public comments, inbound DMs, content and website care. That gives the owner a coherent reputation workflow and a clear next step when a customer question deserves a better answer on the website or in the next post.

Questions buyers ask

What does BabyRock do with WhatsApp?

WhatsApp is the owner's decision thread. BabyRock sends important inbound DM alerts, review drafts, summaries and listing updates there so the owner can approve the next step from the conversation already in hand.

What does BabyRock include for free?

Google review replies are available on the free entry. Public Instagram and Facebook comment replies plus DM alerts are available for smaller accounts. Paid Start and the Grow plan add automated replies to inbound DMs.

How do the paid plans support a growing business?

Start is 9.99 EUR a month for automated inbound replies. The Grow plan is 24.99 EUR for a larger conversation allowance and a second seat. Sensitive decisions remain with the owner.

How are difficult Google reviews handled?

One to three star replies start with the owner for approval. Sensitive cases receive closer attention. Routine four and five star reviews can publish automatically in the account's brand voice.

Can BabyRock create content and care for the website?

Yes, through optional paid services. Content 12 is 9.99 EUR a month for 12 owner-approved publications, Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR, website care is 49.99 EUR, and a supported build or rebuild starts from 499 EUR once.

Does Wati have a free plan?

Wati's WhatsApp product has a 7-day trial. Astra's USD 0 plan is a website widget with 100 monthly credits, and the USD 99 Astra Pro plan adds WhatsApp.
