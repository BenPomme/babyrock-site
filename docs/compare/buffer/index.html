Questions buyers ask

How is BabyRock different from Buffer?

BabyRock centres owner-led public replies, paid inbound DMs, Google reviews, content and website care. Buffer centres social publishing, scheduling and a shared comment workspace.

What does BabyRock handle across comments and DMs?

BabyRock replies to Instagram and Facebook public comments in the brand voice and brings important inbound DMs to the owner. Paid plans automate routine inbound replies, while complaints, negotiations and unusual requests go to the owner.

How do paid inbound DMs work?

Start and Grow automate replies to people who contact the account first. Start is 9.99 EUR a month for a smaller inbound community and Grow is 24.99 EUR a month for a busier account, with sensitive decisions escalated to the owner.

Can BabyRock help with content and my website?

Yes. Content 12 is 9.99 EUR a month for 12 owner-approved publications and Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR a month. Website care is 49.99 EUR a month, and a supported build or rebuild starts from 499 EUR once.

How are sensitive Google reviews handled?

One to three star reviews start as an owner draft, so the owner can approve the reply or pause it. Four and five star praise can move automatically, and sensitive cases stay with the owner's judgment.

Is there a free way to start?

Yes. BabyRock includes a free entry for everyday Google and social engagement, then paid plans for inbound DM automation and optional content and website services.
