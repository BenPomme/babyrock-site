Questions buyers ask

What can BabyRock handle across comments and DMs?

BabyRock replies to Instagram and Facebook public comments in the brand voice, sends alerts for important inbound DMs and automates routine inbound replies on paid plans. Start is 9.99 EUR a month and the Grow plan is 24.99 EUR a month. Complaints, negotiations and unusual requests go to the owner.

How does the owner stay involved?

One WhatsApp thread carries alerts, review decisions, content directions and website requests. BabyRock keeps the routine work moving while the owner approves the moments that need brand judgment or a personal answer.

Can BabyRock publish content and care for the website?

Yes. Content 12 is 9.99 EUR a month for 12 owner-approved publications and Content Unlimited is 29.99 EUR a month. Website care is 49.99 EUR a month, and a supported build or rebuild starts from 499 EUR once.

How are sensitive Google reviews handled?

One to three star reviews start as an owner draft, so the owner can approve the reply or pause it. Four and five star praise can move automatically, and sensitive cases stay with the owner's judgment.

How is BabyRock different from Meta Business Agent?

BabyRock owns the visible customer experience around the brand: public comments, paid inbound DMs, reviews, content and the website. Meta Business Agent handles conversational sales and service inside WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram.

Is there a free way to start?

Yes. BabyRock has a free entry for everyday Google and social engagement, then paid plans for inbound DM automation and optional content and website services.
