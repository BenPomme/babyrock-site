# Copy: the English reference, and native translations

Ben, 28 September 2026: "your french is terribly unauthentic. i believe your spanish too. let's use
english as our reference. make sure this is documented." This file is that documentation, and the
rule applies to every customer-facing sentence in this repository.

## The rule

1. **English is the reference.** Every marketing sentence is written in English first, in a file
   that can be reviewed on its own (`site-v4/templates/*.html` for the public site,
   `src/lib/*-copy.ts` for the app's strings, the Google Docs for approval). Nothing is invented in
   Spanish, French or Catalan that does not exist in the English.
2. **Every other language is written natively, never translated word for word.** Spanish is Spanish
   from Spain, French is French from France, Catalan is Catalan. A sentence that keeps the English
   structure is a defect, even when it is grammatically correct.
3. **Marketing language is the register.** These are startup and marketing pages: short sentences,
   concrete verbs, the customer's own words, no corporate filler, no "solution", no "leverage", no
   "seamless". The marketing register paper is `~/.codex/skills/copywriting/SKILL.md` (conversion
   copywriting), and it is the one to follow when the English itself is written or rewritten.
4. **The translation tool is installed.** `~/.codex/skills/native-localization-translator/SKILL.md`
   (v4.1.1, from `luispittagros/native-translator`) is the native localization skill: it treats
   English as the canonical source, it produces native copy rather than translated copy, and it
   carries quality gates for placeholders, terminology and register. Use it for every ES, FR and CA
   version. Its QA script is `~/.codex/skills/native-localization-translator/scripts/i18n_qa.py`.
5. **Ben approves every public sentence before it ships.** See the approval rule in `AGENTS.md` and
   the ledger `.scratch/copy-approvals.md`. The reference document is the one he edits; a
   translation that he has not seen is a draft.

The fixed words, the banned words and the numbers that cannot drift are in
`docs/agents/copy-glossary.md`. A translation reads that table as its glossary.

## The workflow

1. Write or rewrite the English line. Check the register against `copywriting`.
2. Put the English, and its current translations, in front of Ben in an editable document. The site
   copy decks live in `.scratch/rebuild-v2/copy-decks/`, one Word file and one Google Doc per
   language, generated by `node .scratch/rebuild-v2/copy-decks/extract.mjs` from the built pages.
3. Correct the English first. English is the reference, so a change there is a change everywhere.
4. Redo the affected Spanish, French and Catalan lines with the native localization skill, not by
   editing the old translation.
5. Stage it, and record the approval in `.scratch/copy-approvals.md` with the surface, the date and
   the exact words.

## What "native" rules out, in practice

- No literal calques: "we are committed to" is not "nous nous engageons à" on a shop's landing page,
  and "get started" is not "commencez".
- No English punctuation habits: Spanish uses its own question marks, French uses its own spacing
  before `:` and `?`, and neither uses an em dash. The house style bans em and en dashes everywhere,
  in every language.
- No register jumps inside one page: a landing page talks to a shop owner, not to a procurement
  department.
- No promises that English does not make. The four languages carry the same facts and the same
  fences.

## The words that do not translate

- Product and tier names stay as they are: BabyRock, Lite, Plus, Pro, Enterprise, Rosalía (Rosalia
  without the accent in code and URLs).
- English says **plan**: the free plan, a paid plan, the four plans. Spanish says plan, Catalan says
  pla, French says formule. "level" is never used in customer-facing copy, and "tier" is the internal
  domain word (code, database, tickets), not customer copy. Ben replaced Free Tier and Paid Tier with
  Free Plan and Paid Plan on 29 September 2026.
- The tagline is the same in every language: "BabyRock, your social presence, handled."
- The brands keep their own spelling: Google, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Meta, Stripe.
